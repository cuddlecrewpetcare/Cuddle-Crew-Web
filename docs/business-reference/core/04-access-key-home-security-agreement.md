# Access, Key & Home Security Agreement

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; use when Cuddle Crew will enter or secure the client's home.
> Website use: Access requirements, key/lockbox/smart-lock expectations, cameras, alarms, access failures, and related FAQs.

## Purpose

This agreement documents the client's authorization for Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew") to access the service location and establishes responsibilities for keys, codes, alarms, cameras, home security, and access failures. Client-specific access instructions remain in Precise Pet Care ("PPC") and are not duplicated here.

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Authorization to Enter
The Client authorizes Cuddle Crew to enter the service address for approved services using the access method recorded in PPC. Access may include a client-provided key, lockbox, keypad, smart lock, gate, garage, concierge procedure, or another method specifically accepted by Cuddle Crew.

### 2. Accurate and Workable Access
The Client is responsible for providing safe, lawful, reliable, and tested access before service begins and for keeping access instructions current. Changes to locks, codes, alarms, gates, building procedures, parking requirements, or other access conditions must be updated in PPC before the next scheduled service.

Cuddle Crew may test the primary and backup access method during onboarding or a Meet & Greet. A successful prior test does not guarantee that a device, code, lock, concierge, gate, or other third-party access system will remain functional.

### 3. Keys and Access Devices
Keys and access devices entrusted to Cuddle Crew will be handled with reasonable care. Cuddle Crew will not label a key with the Client's full address or other unnecessary identifying information.

Hidden outdoor keys are not a preferred access method and may be declined when Cuddle Crew believes the location is insecure or unreliable. Key pickup or return outside the normal agreed onboarding process may require a separate trip or fee when permitted by the current Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy.

### 4. Lockboxes, Codes, Smart Locks, Gates, and Alarms
The Client must provide complete instructions for any lockbox, keypad, smart lock, gate, garage, alarm, concierge, leasing-office, or similar system needed to enter or secure the property. The Client is responsible for ensuring batteries, internet-dependent devices, remotes, fobs, codes, and permissions are functional throughout the booking.

If an alarm is accidentally triggered despite following the Client's instructions, Cuddle Crew may contact the Client, emergency contact, alarm company, building representative, or other appropriate party as reasonably necessary.

### 5. Access Failure
If Cuddle Crew cannot safely enter because of inaccurate instructions, changed or nonfunctional access, a missing key, failed lockbox, disabled smart-lock permission, gate/concierge restriction, alarm issue, or another Client-controlled condition, Cuddle Crew will make reasonable efforts to contact the Client or designated contact.

The reserved service may remain chargeable when the failure is outside Cuddle Crew's control. Any separate return trip is subject to availability and the current pricing policies. Cuddle Crew is not required to force entry, climb fences, defeat locks, enter through an unsafe opening, or use another method that is unlawful, unsafe, damaging, or outside the agreed access plan.

### 6. Securing the Home
Cuddle Crew will use reasonable care to follow the Client's current instructions for locking doors, gates, garages, windows, alarms, and other ordinary security steps within the booked service scope. The Client must identify unusual locks, automatic-locking doors, special latches, known defects, or other conditions requiring special handling.

Cuddle Crew is not responsible for a pre-existing defect, failure of a lock/security system, or an act of a third party outside Cuddle Crew's reasonable control.

### 7. Other People With Access
The Client must disclose known household occupants, visitors, contractors, cleaners, relatives, friends, neighbors, other pet-care providers, or other persons expected to enter the property during Cuddle Crew's booking.

If an unexpected person is present and the sitter reasonably believes entry or continued presence may be unsafe, Cuddle Crew may remain outside, leave the property, modify the visit, and contact the Client or emergency contact.

### 8. Cameras, Audio Devices, and Privacy
The Client must disclose indoor cameras and other monitoring devices relevant to the service area. Cameras may be used for legitimate home-security purposes, but devices may not be placed or used in a manner that intrudes on a sitter's reasonable expectation of privacy while changing clothes, bathing, using a bathroom, or sleeping during an overnight service.

Undisclosed or inappropriately placed monitoring equipment may result in service modification or discontinuation when reasonably necessary for privacy or safety.

### 9. Emergencies Affecting the Property
Cuddle Crew may take reasonable, limited steps to protect pets and reduce immediate property damage when an urgent condition is discovered, such as an open door, active leak, loss of climate control, or similar problem. Cuddle Crew may contact the Client, emergency contact, property contact, building management, emergency services, or an appropriate professional when reasonably necessary. The Client remains responsible for repair costs and third-party charges unless caused by Cuddle Crew's legally actionable conduct.

### 10. Client Acknowledgment
By submitting this form, the Client confirms that the access and home-security information maintained in PPC is accurate and current; that all material access hazards, cameras, alarms, occupants, and expected visitors have been disclosed; and that the Client authorizes Cuddle Crew to use the approved access method for scheduled services.
