# Emergency Veterinary Authorization & Care Release

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; required before Cuddle Crew assumes independent pet-care responsibility.
> Website use: General emergency-care expectations only. Never expose client-specific veterinary or payment information publicly.

## Purpose
This form authorizes Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew") to obtain or facilitate veterinary care for a Client's pet when reasonably necessary during an active booking and the Client cannot be reached promptly. Veterinary, emergency-contact, medical, and pet information remains in Precise Pet Care ("PPC").

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Authorization to Seek Veterinary Care
The Client authorizes Cuddle Crew to contact the pet's primary veterinarian, preferred emergency veterinary hospital, another reasonably available veterinary facility, and the Client's designated emergency contacts when Cuddle Crew reasonably believes veterinary evaluation or treatment may be necessary for the pet's welfare.

If the Client cannot be reached within a timeframe reasonably appropriate to the circumstances, the Client authorizes Cuddle Crew to seek or facilitate veterinary evaluation and treatment consistent with this authorization, the pet's PPC record, any spending limit or treatment restriction supplied by the Client, and the veterinarian's professional judgment.

### 2. Release of Veterinary Information
The Client authorizes veterinary providers to disclose to Cuddle Crew information reasonably necessary to discuss the pet's condition, treatment options, discharge instructions, medications, and follow-up care during the booking.

### 3. Financial Responsibility
The Client remains financially responsible for all veterinary services, medications, diagnostics, hospitalization, procedures, transportation, third-party services, and other emergency expenses incurred for the Client's pet, except to the extent a cost is legally attributable to Cuddle Crew.

The Client should maintain an appropriate payment method or payment arrangement directly with the veterinary provider when possible. Full payment-card numbers, CVV codes, or other sensitive card credentials must not be stored in this form or in the website repository.

### 4. Emergency Spending Authorization
The Client must select or provide an emergency spending instruction in PPC/Form Builder. The stated amount is a spending authorization, not a guarantee that a veterinarian can provide all medically appropriate treatment within that amount.

If delaying care to obtain additional authorization could reasonably threaten the pet's life or materially worsen suffering, Cuddle Crew may ask the veterinarian to provide stabilization or other immediately necessary care to the extent permitted by law and the Client's documented instructions.

### 5. Treatment Restrictions and Euthanasia
The Client is responsible for documenting any known treatment restrictions, advance instructions, or exclusions before service begins.

Cuddle Crew does not independently authorize euthanasia except where the Client has expressly provided legally sufficient authorization or where applicable law and the attending veterinarian permit action under an emergency circumstance. When reasonably possible, Cuddle Crew will attempt to reach the Client and designated emergency contacts before any end-of-life decision.

### 6. Transportation
Cuddle Crew does not promise pet transportation merely because emergency veterinary care is authorized. Transportation may be provided only when lawful, safe, within current insurance/business policy, and reasonably feasible. Otherwise, Cuddle Crew may arrange or request an authorized emergency contact, veterinary transport service, rideshare/taxi that permits the animal, emergency responder, or another appropriate transport option.

### 7. No Diagnosis or Guarantee
Cuddle Crew is not a veterinary provider and does not diagnose medical conditions or guarantee treatment outcomes. The sitter may use reasonable first-aid measures within current training and scope while contacting appropriate veterinary or emergency resources.

### 8. Communication During an Emergency
Cuddle Crew may contact the Client using the communication methods recorded in PPC and may contact designated emergency contacts if the Client cannot be reached. The Client is responsible for maintaining current phone numbers, veterinarian information, emergency contacts, and relevant medical instructions.

### 9. Pet Death or Aftercare
If a pet dies during a booking, Cuddle Crew will make reasonable efforts to contact the Client and designated emergency contacts and will follow the Client's documented aftercare instructions when lawful and reasonably practicable. If no instructions are available, Cuddle Crew may seek guidance from the attending veterinary provider and preserve the pet's remains in a reasonable manner until the Client or authorized representative can decide next steps.

### 10. Authorization Period
Unless revoked in writing and acknowledged by Cuddle Crew, this authorization remains applicable to future Cuddle Crew bookings while the Client account remains active. The Client must update the authorization whenever veterinary providers, emergency contacts, spending limits, treatment restrictions, or other material instructions change.

### 11. Client Acknowledgment
By submitting this form, the Client confirms that the veterinary, emergency-contact, medical, spending-limit, and treatment-restriction information supplied to Cuddle Crew is accurate and authorizes Cuddle Crew to act as described above when reasonably necessary for the pet's welfare.

## Form Builder Fields

- Treatment exclusions/limitations, if any.
- Euthanasia instruction/authorization preference.
- Aftercare/remains instructions, if any.
- Authorized alternative transporter/contact, if desired.
- Required acknowledgment checkbox and electronic signature/acceptance.
