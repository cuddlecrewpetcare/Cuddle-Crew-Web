# Medication Administration Consent

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; required when Cuddle Crew is asked to administer medication or supplements.
> Website use: Medication scope, limitations, and personalized-review messaging.

## Purpose
This consent authorizes Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew") to administer medications or supplements according to the current instructions supplied by the Client in Precise Pet Care ("PPC") and establishes the limits of that service.

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Client Authorization
The Client authorizes Cuddle Crew to administer the medications and supplements identified in the pet's current PPC profile and any related approved medication instructions.

The Client is responsible for providing complete and accurate information including medication name, strength, dose, route, timing or acceptable administration window, storage requirements, administration technique, prescribing veterinarian when applicable, known reactions, and instructions for refusal or a missed dose.

### 2. Medication Must Be Properly Supplied
Medication must be supplied in a safe, usable form and, when prescription labeling is applicable, in the original or otherwise properly labeled container unless Cuddle Crew has specifically accepted another arrangement. The Client must provide enough medication for the booking plus a reasonable contingency supply when practical.

Cuddle Crew will not independently change a dose, route, frequency, prescription, or treatment plan.

### 3. Routine Scope
Routine oral or topical medications may be accepted when the sitter can administer them safely, the pet cooperates sufficiently, the care fits within the booked service, and the task is within Cuddle Crew's current training, insurance, and approved service scope.

Eye/ear medication, injections, exact-time or high-consequence medication, complex medical regimens, specialized procedures, or care requiring substantial restraint are subject to individual review before acceptance.

### 4. Refusal and Unsafe Restraint
The Client understands that successful administration cannot be guaranteed. If a pet hides, refuses medication, spits out a dose, vomits, becomes distressed, attempts to bite/scratch, or otherwise cannot be medicated safely, Cuddle Crew will not escalate to forceful or unsafe restraint merely to complete the task.

Cuddle Crew may attempt reasonable Client-approved alternatives, contact the Client or emergency contact, and seek veterinary guidance when appropriate. The event will be documented in the care record when practicable.

### 5. Timing
Cuddle Crew will make reasonable efforts to administer medication within the accepted timing instructions. An exact clock-time guarantee exists only when Cuddle Crew has expressly reviewed and accepted that requirement before booking.

Medication whose safety or effectiveness depends on strict timing may require personalized review, schedule modification, a different service plan, or referral to another provider.

### 6. Changes During a Booking
The Client must promptly communicate medication changes through the approved business communication channel and update PPC. Cuddle Crew may require written veterinary instructions or additional review before implementing a material medication change.

Instructions relayed only through an informal third party are not automatically accepted.

### 7. Adverse Reactions and Emergencies
If Cuddle Crew observes a concerning reaction, suspected overdose, medication error, or significant change in condition, Cuddle Crew may contact the Client, emergency contact, veterinarian, poison-control resource, or emergency veterinary facility and act under the Client's Emergency Veterinary Authorization.

### 8. Additional Time or Scope
If medication administration consistently requires more time than can reasonably fit within the booked service, Cuddle Crew may require a longer service or revised care plan. Payment does not require Cuddle Crew to perform a procedure that is unsafe, unlawful, uninsured, or outside approved scope.

### 9. Client Responsibility
The Client represents that the medication instructions supplied are current and appropriate for the pet and accepts responsibility for consequences arising from inaccurate instructions, improperly supplied medication, undisclosed medical conditions, or changes the Client failed to communicate, except to the extent legally caused by Cuddle Crew.

### 10. Client Acknowledgment
By submitting this form, the Client authorizes medication administration within the limits above and confirms that the medication information maintained in PPC is complete and current.
