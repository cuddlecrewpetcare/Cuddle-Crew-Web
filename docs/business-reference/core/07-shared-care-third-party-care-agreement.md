# Shared Care / Third-Party Care Agreement

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; required when another person or provider shares pet/home-care responsibility during the same booking period.
> Website use: Shared-care disclosure and coordination expectations.

## Purpose
This agreement documents a shared-care arrangement when someone other than Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew") will also enter the home, care for the pet, administer medication, feed, walk, transport, or otherwise assume responsibility during Cuddle Crew's booking period.

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Required Disclosure
The Client must identify all known third-party caregivers or providers expected to share responsibility during the booking, including household members, relatives, friends, neighbors, cleaners, dog walkers, boarding/daycare providers, or other pet sitters when their activities may affect Cuddle Crew's care.

The Client must provide a workable division of responsibilities. A person who may or may not stop by cannot be relied upon as a required part of the pet's welfare plan unless the Client establishes a clear schedule and responsibility.

### 2. Division of Duties
The Client is responsible for specifying which provider is responsible for meals, medication, walks, potty/litter/enclosure care, overnight presence, transportation, household tasks, and other material duties.

Cuddle Crew is responsible only for the services Cuddle Crew has accepted and scheduled. Cuddle Crew does not silently assume another caregiver's missed or incomplete duties.

### 3. Third-Party Acts and Omissions
Cuddle Crew cannot control and is not responsible for the independent acts, omissions, errors, property changes, access/security actions, feeding, medication administration, handling decisions, or other conduct of third parties, except to the extent Cuddle Crew is legally responsible for its own conduct.

If Cuddle Crew discovers a condition apparently caused or altered by another caregiver, Cuddle Crew may document the condition and contact the Client or emergency contact.

### 4. Missed Third-Party Care
If Cuddle Crew reasonably believes another caregiver failed to complete an important responsibility, Cuddle Crew may contact the Client or emergency contact and, when animal welfare reasonably requires it, may provide limited additional care if safe, lawful, within scope, and feasible.

Additional visits, extended time, supplies, transportation, or other work are not automatically included and may be chargeable under the current pricing policies when authorized or reasonably necessary in an emergency.

### 5. Access and Security
The Client must ensure that all caregivers have compatible access instructions and understand any locking, gate, alarm, camera, pet-confinement, or security requirements relevant to their role. Cuddle Crew is not responsible for ensuring another provider locks the home, arms an alarm, closes a gate, or follows the Client's instructions.

### 6. Care Records and Communication
Cuddle Crew will document its own completed services through its normal care-journal process. Cuddle Crew does not verify or certify care reportedly completed by another provider.

The Client should avoid conflicting instructions from multiple people. Unless Cuddle Crew expressly agrees otherwise, the Client or the Client's designated primary contact remains the person authorized to make routine changes to Cuddle Crew's instructions.

### 7. Medication and Health-Care Coordination
When multiple caregivers administer medication or health-related care, the Client is responsible for establishing a clear method to prevent missed or duplicate doses. Cuddle Crew may decline a medication plan when the division of responsibility is too uncertain to manage safely.

### 8. Safety and Service Modification
Cuddle Crew may modify, pause, or discontinue service if third-party activity creates unsafe handling, unreliable access, material confusion about the pet's care, repeated interference with scheduled service, or another condition that prevents Cuddle Crew from providing care responsibly.

### 9. Client Acknowledgment
By submitting this form, the Client confirms that all known shared-care participants have been disclosed; that responsibilities have been divided clearly; and that the Client understands Cuddle Crew is responsible for Cuddle Crew's accepted services, not for independently performed third-party care.

## Form Builder Fields
- Other caregiver/provider name and relationship/company.
- Contact information.
- Dates/times or general schedule.
- Specific responsibilities.
- Medication responsibility, if applicable.
- Home-access overlap/visitor notes.
- Required acknowledgment and electronic signature/acceptance.

## Website Implementation Notes
- Do not require this form when there is no shared-care arrangement.
- Website intake may ask whether anyone else will provide care during the booking and trigger this form when yes.
