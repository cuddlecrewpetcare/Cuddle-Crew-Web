# Overnight Care Addendum

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; required for Overnight Care clients.
> Website use: Overnight scope, home requirements, privacy, and daytime-care clarification.

## Purpose
This addendum supplements the Master Service Agreement for bookings that include Overnight Care by Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew"). Client-specific sleeping, access, household, and pet-care instructions remain in Precise Pet Care ("PPC").

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Overnight Service Window
Standard Overnight Care generally covers approximately **6:00 PM through 8:00 AM**. It is an overnight presence/service window and is **not 24-hour continuous care**.

Arrival and departure may occur within the approved overnight framework rather than at an exact minute unless an exact-time need has been specifically reviewed and accepted.

### 2. Daytime Care
Daytime care outside the overnight window is separate unless expressly included in the confirmed booking. A standard midday add-on is one separate 30-minute daytime care visit. Longer daytime care or walks use the applicable current service and pricing structure.

The Client is responsible for booking enough daytime care to meet the pets' feeding, medication, elimination, exercise, welfare, and alone-time needs.

### 3. Suitable Sleeping and Bathroom Facilities
The Client will provide a safe, reasonably sanitary sleeping area and clean bedding or another mutually agreed sleeping arrangement, together with reasonable access to a functioning bathroom, running water, electricity, and appropriate heating/cooling.

Cuddle Crew may decline or discontinue an overnight arrangement when the home environment is materially unsafe, unsanitary, without essential utilities, or otherwise unsuitable for a sitter to remain overnight.

### 4. Occupants and Visitors
All household occupants and known expected overnight visitors must be disclosed before service. Unexpected occupants or visitors may cause Cuddle Crew to delay entry, leave, or contact the Client when the sitter reasonably believes the situation may be unsafe or materially different from the accepted booking.

### 5. Cameras and Privacy
Indoor cameras and relevant monitoring devices must be disclosed. Cameras or audio devices may not be placed or used in areas where the sitter reasonably expects privacy while sleeping, changing clothes, bathing, or using a bathroom.

If undisclosed monitoring or a privacy concern is discovered, Cuddle Crew may reposition itself to a private area when possible, contact the Client, or discontinue the overnight portion if necessary.

### 6. Access, Parking, and After-Dark Safety
The Client must provide safe, reliable access and any necessary parking, gate, building, concierge, alarm, or after-dark instructions. Cuddle Crew is not required to park illegally, enter through an unsafe route, or remain in circumstances the sitter reasonably believes present a personal-safety risk.

### 7. Communication Connectivity
Reasonable Wi-Fi or cellular connectivity should be available for care journals, Client communication, and emergency contact. If service is known to be unavailable, the Client and Cuddle Crew should agree on an alternative communication plan before the booking when practicable.

### 8. Household Tasks
Only ordinary incidental household tasks specifically accepted as part of the booking are included. Typical examples may include bringing in mail, reasonable trash/recycling handling, alternating agreed lights/window treatments, or basic plant watering when agreed.

Overnight Care does not automatically include housekeeping, extensive cleaning, yard work, home maintenance, large package management, pool maintenance, snow/ice work, care for undisclosed animals, or other substantial property services.

### 9. Food and Personal Items
Unless otherwise agreed, the sitter may bring personal food and belongings needed for the overnight stay. The Client should disclose material kitchen restrictions, allergies within the household, alarmed/restricted areas, or other relevant home-use limitations.

### 10. Home Emergencies
The Client should provide an appropriate home/property emergency contact when another person may need to make decisions about utilities, leaks, HVAC, locks, or property repairs. Cuddle Crew may take reasonable limited steps to protect pets and reduce immediate damage but does not become the Client's property manager.

### 11. Pet Sleeping Arrangements
The Client must disclose the pets' normal overnight confinement and sleeping routine. Cuddle Crew may modify sleeping proximity or handling when reasonably necessary for sitter safety while preserving appropriate pet care.

### 12. Service Suitability
Overnight bookings remain subject to behavior, medication, travel, schedule, home-environment, and overall suitability review. Seven or more consecutive nights may receive an additional long-stay review for sustainability; length alone does not automatically create a discount or surcharge.

### 13. Client Acknowledgment
By submitting this addendum, the Client confirms that the overnight environment and instructions disclosed in PPC are accurate and that the Client understands the overnight service window, separate daytime-care needs, home requirements, and privacy expectations described above.
