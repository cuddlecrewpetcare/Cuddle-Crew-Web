# Pet Behavior & Handling Safety Agreement

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; may be required for onboarding and may be re-required when a pet's behavior/risk profile materially changes.
> Website use: Behavior disclosure, safe-handling expectations, and personalized-review triggers.

## Purpose
This agreement confirms the Client's duty to disclose relevant pet behavior and authorizes Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew") to use reasonable safety discretion. Detailed behavior history and handling instructions remain in the pet's Precise Pet Care ("PPC") profile.

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Full Behavior Disclosure
The Client must disclose known behavior that could affect safe care, including bite, snap, scratch, lunge, aggression, resource guarding, reactivity, fear, escape/door-dashing, fence jumping, leash-related behavior, handling sensitivity, conflict with people or animals, and other material incidents or triggers.

The Client must update Cuddle Crew before the next service if behavior materially changes or a new incident occurs.

### 2. No Guarantee of Behavior
Animals can behave differently when owners are away, routines change, strangers enter the home, pain or illness develops, or environmental conditions change. Cuddle Crew cannot guarantee that a pet will behave as previously observed or described.

### 3. Safety Discretion
Cuddle Crew may create distance, use barriers, avoid a trigger, shorten or modify an activity, change handling technique, decline physical contact, discontinue a task, or leave an unsafe area when reasonably necessary to protect the pet, sitter, another animal, or another person.

Payment or a booked service does not require Cuddle Crew to continue unsafe handling.

### 4. Leashing and Walks
A dog that cannot be safely leashed, harnessed, exited from the home, or controlled with the Client-provided approved equipment will not be forced into a walk. When authorized and safe, Cuddle Crew may instead provide an appropriate alternative such as a potty opportunity or indoor/backyard care within the booked time.

Cuddle Crew will not intentionally provide off-leash walking in an unfenced public area merely because the Client reports that the dog normally walks off leash.

### 5. Equipment
The Client is responsible for providing properly fitted and functional collars, harnesses, leashes, carriers, gates, crates, and other required equipment. Cuddle Crew may decline to use equipment believed to be damaged, unsafe, inappropriate, or outside accepted handling scope.

### 6. Restraint and Medication
Cuddle Crew does not use escalating force or unsafe restraint solely to complete grooming-like handling, medication, leashing, or another non-emergency task. If a pet's resistance creates a meaningful bite, scratch, escape, or injury risk, Cuddle Crew may stop and follow the approved alternative/contact plan.

### 7. Animal-to-Animal Interaction
Pets will not be introduced to unfamiliar animals or combined with another household's pets merely for convenience. Client instructions regarding separation within the household must be accurate and workable.

### 8. Serious Behavior History
Known serious bite history, severe aggression, materially elevated escape risk, or another high-risk condition requires individualized review before service. Cuddle Crew may require additional precautions, a modified plan, a behavior professional/veterinary recommendation, or may decline service when the risk cannot be managed within current training, insurance, and scope.

### 9. Injury or Incident
If a behavioral incident causes or threatens injury, Cuddle Crew may obtain emergency assistance, contact the Client/emergency contact, separate animals when safely possible, seek veterinary care under the Emergency Veterinary Authorization, or contact emergency/public-safety resources when reasonably necessary.

### 10. Client Responsibility for Accurate Information
The Client acknowledges that failure to disclose known behavior or prior incidents can materially affect Cuddle Crew's ability to plan safe care. The Client is responsible for losses caused by inaccurate or withheld information to the extent permitted by law, except to the extent Cuddle Crew is legally responsible for its own conduct.

### 11. Client Acknowledgment
By submitting this form, the Client confirms that the pet's behavior and incident history in PPC is complete and current and agrees that Cuddle Crew may modify or stop a task when reasonably necessary for safety.
