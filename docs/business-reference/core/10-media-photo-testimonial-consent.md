# Media, Photo & Testimonial Consent

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; required response, optional consent.
> Website use: Media permission and testimonial-use rules.

## Purpose
This form records whether the Client gives Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew") permission to use photographs, video, and optional testimonials for business and promotional purposes. A Client may decline without affecting eligibility for ordinary pet-care services.

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Pet Photo and Video Choice
The Client must select one option:

- **YES — I give permission.** Cuddle Crew may use photographs or video of my pet(s), created during Cuddle Crew services or supplied by me for this purpose, in Cuddle Crew's website, social media, digital/print marketing, educational business content, portfolio, or similar business materials.
- **NO — I do not give permission.** Cuddle Crew may still take photographs or video when reasonably necessary for private care journals, incident documentation, veterinary/emergency communication, or internal business records, but will not intentionally use those materials for public promotional purposes.

### 2. Privacy Protections
Public media should focus on the pet and should not intentionally disclose the Client's private home address, access information, alarm/security details, travel dates, financial information, private medical information, or other unnecessary confidential Client information.

Cuddle Crew should avoid publishing identifiable house numbers, access codes, documents, computer screens, mail, prescription labels, or other sensitive background details when reasonably apparent.

### 3. Client/Household Members
Permission to use pet media does not automatically grant permission to publish identifiable images, voice recordings, names, or testimonials of the Client, household members, or guests. Separate permission should be obtained when a person is a meaningful identifiable subject of promotional content.

### 4. Testimonials and Reviews
If the Client voluntarily provides a testimonial, review, message, or feedback, Cuddle Crew must not materially alter its meaning. Permission to republish a testimonial should be recorded before using private feedback in promotional materials unless the Client has already posted the review publicly in a context where ordinary business sharing/reposting is reasonably expected.

When practical, Cuddle Crew may identify a testimonial by first name and pet name, initials, or another privacy-conscious format rather than publishing unnecessary personal details.

### 5. No Compensation or Service Condition
Unless separately agreed in writing, media permission does not entitle the Client to compensation, discounts, royalties, or ownership of Cuddle Crew's finished marketing materials. Declining promotional permission will not increase the Client's rates or reduce ordinary service quality.

### 6. Withdrawal of Future Permission
The Client may change future media permission by updating the form or contacting Cuddle Crew. Withdrawal applies prospectively after Cuddle Crew receives and can reasonably process the request. Cuddle Crew will make reasonable efforts to stop new promotional use and remove media from Cuddle Crew-controlled digital channels when practicable, but cannot guarantee recall of printed materials, previously distributed content, third-party reposts, cached content, or materials outside Cuddle Crew's control.

### 7. Client Acknowledgment
The Client's selected YES/NO response is the controlling promotional-media preference recorded by Cuddle Crew unless later updated.

## Form Builder Fields
- Required YES/NO promotional photo/video permission.
- Optional testimonial/review republication permission if desired as a separate question.
- Optional preferred public identification (pet name only / first name + pet / anonymous).

## Website Implementation Notes
- Consent must not be preselected.
- Do not condition normal service on promotional consent.
- Keep private care-journal/documentation media separate from promotional permission.
