# Vacation Care Plan / Care-Frequency Approval

> Status: DRAFT — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW
> Classification: CLIENT-FACING / FORM BUILDER
> PPC placement: Form Builder; use for multi-day vacation/absence care, shared-care plans, or care-frequency exceptions requiring explicit approval.
> Website use: Care-frequency expectations and personalized-review messaging.

## Purpose
This form confirms the in-person care plan for a Client's absence and helps prevent misunderstandings about first/last visits, daily frequency, long gaps, other caregivers, meals, medication, and welfare needs. Precise Pet Care ("PPC") remains the source of truth for the confirmed service schedule and pet-care instructions.

## Authoritative Content

### 1. Confirmed Care Plan
The Client must review the booked or proposed care pattern for the absence, including the first service, final service, expected daily visit frequency, overnight services when applicable, and any other caregiver involved.

Submission of this form does not itself reserve calendar space or modify a booking. The schedule shown in PPC after approval controls the services Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC ("Cuddle Crew") has accepted.

### 2. Minimum Welfare Standards
Cuddle Crew may require a different visit frequency, longer service, overnight arrangement, or other care plan when the requested plan does not reasonably meet the pet's basic welfare, medication, elimination, feeding, safety, or supervision needs within Cuddle Crew's service model.

Cats generally require at least one in-person care visit per day during an owner's absence. Dogs generally require multiple daily in-person care opportunities when no responsible person is otherwise present, with the specific plan based on the dog's routine and needs.

A Client request or willingness to pay does not require Cuddle Crew to accept a care frequency Cuddle Crew reasonably considers unsafe or inadequate.

### 3. First and Final Service
The Client is responsible for accurately stating when the pet will last receive care before Cuddle Crew begins and when the Client or another responsible caregiver will resume care after Cuddle Crew's final service.

The Client should notify Cuddle Crew promptly if departure or return plans change in a way that affects pet care. Additional service remains subject to availability and applicable pricing.

### 4. Longest Care Gap
The Client must disclose the expected longest period the pet will go without in-person care and any material constraints such as medication windows, meal timing, elimination needs, senior/medical needs, or separation distress.

Cuddle Crew may require personalized review when the proposed gap is inconsistent with the pet's disclosed needs or normal routine.

### 5. Other Caregivers
If another person or provider shares responsibility, that arrangement must be disclosed and may also require the Shared Care / Third-Party Care Agreement. The Client must clearly identify the other caregiver's responsibilities rather than assuming Cuddle Crew will cover any missed third-party care automatically.

### 6. Overnight Care
When Overnight Care is booked, the Client acknowledges that the standard overnight service generally covers approximately 6:00 PM–8:00 AM and is not 24-hour continuous care. Daytime needs outside that window must be addressed separately in the approved care plan.

### 7. Supplies
The Client must provide adequate food, medication, litter/enclosure supplies, cleaning supplies, and other pet-care necessities for the entire absence plus a reasonable contingency amount when practicable.

If an essential supply unexpectedly runs out, Cuddle Crew may contact the Client/emergency contact and may make a reasonable authorized purchase or arrange another solution when necessary for pet welfare. Applicable pass-through costs or additional trips may be charged under the current pricing policies.

### 8. Delayed Return
If the Client is delayed and no responsible caregiver has resumed care, the Client must contact Cuddle Crew as soon as possible. Cuddle Crew will make reasonable efforts to provide or arrange additional care, but additional service is subject to sitter availability, safety, routing capacity, and current pricing; it is not guaranteed merely because the original booking occurred.

### 9. Emergency Guardianship
For extended absences, the Client should maintain an emergency contact capable of making timely decisions. The separate emergency pet-guardianship concept may be used when the Client wants to designate a person to assume ongoing care if the Client is unexpectedly unable to return, but Cuddle Crew does not become the pet's permanent guardian merely because a booking extends unexpectedly.

### 10. Client Approval
By submitting this form, the Client confirms that the care-frequency information is accurate, understands the longest anticipated gaps in care, identifies other caregivers where applicable, and approves the care plan reflected in the confirmed PPC schedule.

## Form Builder Fields
- Absence/trip dates.
- Last care before Cuddle Crew begins.
- First Cuddle Crew service.
- Daily care pattern / overnight pattern.
- Other caregiver(s) and responsibilities.
- Longest expected gap between in-person care.
- Meal/medication timing constraints.
- Special welfare concerns.
- Expected return/resumption of care.
- Required approval and electronic signature/acceptance.
