## Purpose

This document defines the internal readiness checks that should be completed before a new Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC client begins routine service.

It is intended to prevent incomplete onboarding, missing safety information, untested access, unresolved review flags, payment problems, or other avoidable issues from reaching the first scheduled Service.

This document is an internal operational reference. It may inform website and onboarding behavior, but the checklist itself, internal review status, and private decision logic should not be exposed publicly.

Precise Petcare should remain the operational source of truth for current client-specific profiles, care instructions, access information, authorizations, schedules, and booking records.

## Authoritative Content

# NEW CLIENT PRE-SERVICE CHECKLIST

Before a new Client begins routine Service, confirm each applicable item below.

## 1. Meet & Greet / Onboarding

- [ ] Complimentary Meet & Greet and onboarding have been completed.

**OR**

- [ ] A rare exception to the normal Meet & Greet/onboarding requirement has been personally reviewed and approved.

Additional rules:

- The normal initial Meet & Greet is complimentary.
- Completion of a website form, estimate, quote request, or account signup does not by itself replace the Meet & Greet/onboarding requirement.
- Any exception should be deliberate rather than automatically inferred by software.
- If onboarding remains incomplete, routine Service should not be treated as fully ready unless an approved exception exists.

## 2. Client Intake and Contact Information

- [ ] Client intake information is complete.
- [ ] Primary contact information is complete and current.
- [ ] Service address is confirmed.
- [ ] Emergency contact information is complete.
- [ ] Relevant household occupants, expected visitors, or third-party caregivers have been disclosed where applicable.

Client-specific information should be maintained in Precise Petcare rather than duplicated into a separate public website record system.

## 3. Pet Profiles

- [ ] Every pet receiving care has a current pet profile.
- [ ] All pets and species present in the care plan have been disclosed.
- [ ] Relevant medical conditions, allergies, sensitivities, medications, and behavior information have been recorded.
- [ ] Feeding, water, elimination, exercise/enrichment, and other routine care information is sufficiently documented.
- [ ] Relevant separation, confinement, escape, handling, or compatibility needs are documented.

Do not treat an incomplete pet profile as ready merely because pricing can be calculated.

## 4. Veterinary and Emergency Information

- [ ] Primary veterinary information is complete.
- [ ] Preferred emergency veterinary information is complete where applicable.
- [ ] Emergency-contact information is complete.
- [ ] Emergency authority and related authorization requirements are complete where applicable.

Do not assume veterinary authority from a general booking request or website form.

## 5. Required Agreements and Authorizations

- [ ] Master Service Agreement has been accepted.
- [ ] Current Cancellation, Booking Change & Refund Policy has been accepted.
- [ ] Current Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy has been accepted.
- [ ] Any additional required Client-facing agreement, authorization, addendum, or consent applicable to the booking has been completed.

Examples of conditional documents may include:

- Access, Key & Home Security Agreement;
- Emergency Veterinary Authorization & Care Release;
- Medication Administration Consent & Instructions;
- Shared Care / Third-Party Care Agreement;
- Overnight Care Addendum & Home Requirements;
- Pet Behavior & Handling Disclosure / Safety Agreement;
- Media, Photo & Testimonial Consent;
- Vacation Care Plan / Care-Frequency Approval;
- or another approved Client-facing document applicable to the requested care.

Conditional documents should be required only where relevant to the Client’s actual Services or circumstances.

## 6. Medication and Medical-Scope Review

Where medication or medical care is involved:

- [ ] Medication details are complete.
- [ ] Required medication consent is complete.
- [ ] Administration instructions are sufficiently clear.
- [ ] Timing requirements and acceptable timing windows are understood.
- [ ] Medication or medical-care needs have been confirmed to be within Cuddle Crew’s approved training, insurance, safety, and service scope.
- [ ] Any advanced, high-risk, exact-time, unusually time-intensive, or otherwise complex medication needs have completed the appropriate scope review.

Unresolved medication-scope concerns prevent automatic pre-service approval.

## 7. Behavior and Safety Review

- [ ] Relevant behavior and handling disclosures have been reviewed.
- [ ] Bite, snap, guarding, reactivity, escape, door-dashing, handling-sensitivity, or separation concerns are understood where applicable.
- [ ] Required handling equipment is known and workable.
- [ ] Any materially elevated behavior or safety concern has completed the appropriate internal review.

Payment, onboarding completion, or a calculated price must not override unresolved safety concerns.

## 8. Access and Home Readiness

- [ ] Primary access method is documented.
- [ ] Backup access method is documented where applicable.
- [ ] Relevant keys, codes, lockboxes, gates, alarms, concierge/leasing restrictions, or smart-lock instructions are documented.
- [ ] Access method has been tested before the first routine Service.
- [ ] Relevant camera/audio-device information has been disclosed where required.
- [ ] Known parking or building-access requirements are workable.
- [ ] Access does not currently present an unresolved safety or operational problem.

A planned access method should not be considered ready merely because instructions were submitted; it should be tested before the first routine Service.

## 9. Care Frequency and Service Duration

- [ ] Requested care frequency has been reviewed and is considered adequate for the pet’s welfare.
- [ ] Requested Service duration realistically fits the complete care routine.
- [ ] Any separate daytime, overnight, walk, medication, feeding, elimination, enrichment, or other needs have been accounted for.
- [ ] If the requested routine does not reasonably fit the selected duration, a longer Service, additional Service, revised plan, or personalized review has been completed.

An automatic feeder, fountain, camera, self-cleaning litter box, or other device may support a care plan but does not automatically replace appropriate in-person welfare checks.

## 10. Schedule and Booking Details

- [ ] Requested Service dates have been reviewed.
- [ ] Applicable Service duration(s) have been confirmed.
- [ ] Applicable arrival window(s) or schedule expectations have been confirmed.
- [ ] Any exact-time requirement has been separately reviewed and accepted where applicable.
- [ ] Final booking scope has been confirmed.
- [ ] Final price has been confirmed.
- [ ] The booking is actually confirmed rather than remaining a preliminary quote or request.

A website estimate, quote request, or mathematically calculable price does not by itself mean the booking is accepted.

## 11. Travel Zone and Route Feasibility

- [ ] Service-area eligibility has been reviewed.
- [ ] Applicable travel zone has been confirmed.
- [ ] Any applicable travel fee has been identified.
- [ ] Route feasibility has been reviewed.
- [ ] The booking is operationally workable within available schedule and route capacity.

For daytime Services, travel-zone classification should follow the current Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy.

Overnight Services outside the Standard Zone require individualized review rather than automatic application of the daytime travel-fee schedule.

## 12. Holiday, Peak-Date, and Short-Notice Review

- [ ] Applicable holiday or peak-date treatment has been checked.
- [ ] Applicable short-notice or same-day treatment has been checked.
- [ ] Any applicable modifier has been disclosed before confirmation where required.
- [ ] Holiday/peak-date treatment comes from the current approved holiday/peak-date reference rather than being invented by website logic.

Short-notice or holiday pricing does not guarantee booking availability.

## 13. Payment and Reservation Requirements

- [ ] Applicable payment deadline has been established.
- [ ] Any required reservation payment has been disclosed.
- [ ] Any payment or reservation requirement due at the current stage has been satisfied.
- [ ] Any approved alternative payment arrangement has been documented.

Important distinctions:

- Payment timing must follow the current Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy and confirmed booking/invoice.
- The ordinary payment deadline is not the same thing as a cancellation deadline.
- A reservation payment is not automatically non-refundable.
- Payment does not override safety, scope, availability, or booking-acceptance requirements.
- A payment received for a booking that still requires human review must not automatically convert the booking into an approved Service.

## 14. Backup / Continuity Authorization

- [ ] Client authorization or decline regarding use of an approved vetted backup provider or emergency substitute has been recorded where applicable.
- [ ] Any applicable continuity pathway is documented.
- [ ] No private access information, medical information, or Client security information will be shared with an unauthorized provider.

The website should not expose internal backup-provider identities, credentials, access information, or continuity details unless expressly approved.

## 15. Review Flags

Before Service begins, determine whether any condition requires additional review.

Potential review conditions include:

- unusually large pet households;
- multiple materially distinct care routines;
- advanced or high-risk medication;
- exact-time care;
- significant behavior or safety concerns;
- complex separation requirements;
- unusual species;
- continuous-care requests;
- transportation requests;
- substantial travel;
- complex or extended-area Overnight Care;
- care that may not fit within the standard maximum daytime duration;
- or another material deviation from the standard Service structure.

- [ ] No unresolved review flags remain.

**OR**

- [ ] Applicable review flags have been resolved and the approved conditions are documented.

Internal review thresholds and scoring logic should not be displayed publicly.

## 16. Final Pre-Service Status

Select one:

- [ ] **READY — Approved for Service**
- [ ] **PENDING — Additional information or action required**
- [ ] **PERSONALIZED REVIEW REQUIRED**
- [ ] **MODIFY SERVICE PLAN**
- [ ] **REFER / OUT OF SCOPE**
- [ ] **DECLINE**

### Outstanding Issue(s)

Record internally:

- missing information;
- incomplete forms;
- unsigned/unfinished authorizations;
- access issues;
- payment issues;
- review conditions;
- schedule issues;
- unresolved safety concerns;
- or other pre-service blockers.

### Approval Record

Record:

- approved by;
- approval date;
- applicable conditions;
- and any required follow-up.

## Pre-Service Readiness Rule

A new Client should not be treated as fully ready for routine Service merely because one part of onboarding is complete.

Pre-service readiness requires the applicable onboarding, information, safety, scope, access, scheduling, pricing, authorization, payment, and review requirements to be complete or deliberately resolved.

Where a requirement does not apply to the booking, it may be recorded as not applicable rather than treated as incomplete.

Where a material issue remains unresolved, the appropriate state is **Pending**, **Personalized review required**, **Modify**, **Refer**, or **Decline** rather than automatic approval.

## Website Implementation Notes

This internal checklist may inform website and onboarding behavior, but the public website should expose only the minimum client-facing information necessary to complete onboarding.

### Recommended Website / Portal States

Where technically useful, onboarding may use internal status states such as:

- `not_started`
- `in_progress`
- `pending_client`
- `pending_review`
- `ready`
- `not_approved`

These are implementation examples, not required public labels.

A client-facing interface should use clearer language such as:

- **Action needed**
- **Information needed**
- **Under review**
- **Ready for service**
- **Personalized review required**

Do not expose private internal decline logic.

### Website Onboarding Progress

A client-facing onboarding checklist may safely show high-level completion categories such as:

- Account / contact information
- Pet profiles
- Care information
- Required agreements
- Emergency information
- Access information
- Meet & Greet
- Booking confirmation
- Payment requirement

The website should not show sensitive internal evaluation categories such as:

- risk score;
- behavior risk rating;
- route burden;
- capacity calculation;
- internal acceptance threshold;
- sitter safety assessment;
- decline reason;
- or internal notes.

### Precise Petcare Handoff

The website must not become a second authoritative client-record system.

Where the website collects preliminary inquiry information:

1. use it for inquiry, estimation, or onboarding assistance;
2. direct the Client into the approved Precise Petcare workflow for authoritative Client and pet records;
3. do not treat preliminary website data as automatically approved care instructions;
4. do not silently overwrite current Precise Petcare records;
5. do not expose Precise Petcare data publicly.

### Booking Logic

Website logic must distinguish:

- inquiry;
- estimate;
- onboarding;
- quote;
- booking request;
- pending review;
- confirmed booking;
- and pre-service readiness.

These states are not interchangeable.

A Client should not see a definitive **Ready for service** result if a required human review, Meet & Greet, agreement, authorization, access test, payment requirement, or other applicable blocker remains incomplete.

### Access

Website submission of an access method does not count as successful access testing.

A system may track:

- access information received;
- access test pending;
- access test passed;
- access issue requiring follow-up.

Sensitive access credentials should remain in the approved secure operational system rather than public website code, analytics, logs, or URLs.

### Payment

Website payment logic must remain consistent with the current Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy.

Do not:

- infer a reservation-payment amount;
- assume every booking requires the same reservation payment;
- treat payment as automatic booking acceptance;
- treat payment as proof that all onboarding requirements are complete;
- or automatically cancel a confirmed booking solely because a payment deadline is missed.

### Review Logic

If website automation encounters a condition requiring human review, the public result should ordinarily be:

**Personalized review required**

The website should not explain the underlying internal threshold unless that specific rule has been approved for public disclosure.

### Data Minimization and Privacy

Do not place the following in public-facing logs, frontend code, URLs, analytics events, or browser-visible configuration:

- keys or access codes;
- alarm information;
- private Client travel dates;
- detailed medical information;
- veterinary payment information;
- emergency spending instructions;
- private behavior-risk notes;
- internal review results;
- backup-provider access credentials;
- private service-area origin/reference location.

### Conflict Handling

If website onboarding behavior conflicts with this CURRENT / APPROVED internal reference:

1. flag the conflict;
2. identify the affected feature or workflow;
3. preserve the business rule in this document;
4. do not silently weaken or remove a readiness requirement to make the website flow easier.

If this document conflicts with a CURRENT / APPROVED Client-facing agreement or policy, the Client-facing agreement or policy controls the contractual subject and the conflict should be flagged for human review.
