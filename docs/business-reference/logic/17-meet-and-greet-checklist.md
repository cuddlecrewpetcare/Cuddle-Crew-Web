## Purpose

This document defines the internal review process for the initial Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC Meet & Greet.

The Meet & Greet is used to verify that the information provided during onboarding matches the actual pets, home, access setup, equipment, care routine, and requested Services before a booking is fully accepted.

It is also used to identify unresolved safety, scope, scheduling, access, medication, behavior, pricing, or care-plan issues that require follow-up before Service begins.

The initial standard Meet & Greet is complimentary and is generally expected as part of onboarding unless a rare exception is personally approved.

This document is an internal operational reference. The public website may explain what Clients should expect during a Meet & Greet, but it should not expose private evaluation criteria, internal risk logic, or acceptance reasoning.

Precise Petcare should remain the operational source of truth for current Client, pet, care, access, and booking records.

## Authoritative Content

# MEET & GREET CHECKLIST

Before accepting a new Client booking, complete the applicable Meet & Greet review below.

## 1. Verify Pets and Household Scope

- [ ] Verify every pet expected to receive care.
- [ ] Confirm that all pets and species have been disclosed.
- [ ] Confirm that pet profiles correspond to the actual animals present.
- [ ] Identify any undisclosed pets, animals, enclosures, or care responsibilities.
- [ ] Confirm relevant separation or compatibility needs among household pets.

If the actual household materially differs from the information provided during onboarding, the booking may require updated pricing, a different Service duration, additional documentation, or personalized review.

## 2. Review Feeding, Water, and Daily Care Routine

For each applicable pet:

- [ ] Review feeding routine.
- [ ] Review food location and preparation instructions.
- [ ] Review water routine and water locations.
- [ ] Review potty, litter, enclosure, or elimination routine.
- [ ] Review normal exercise and enrichment needs.
- [ ] Review expected companionship or monitoring needs.
- [ ] Review agreed cleanup tasks.
- [ ] Confirm any Client-approved alternatives if the normal routine cannot be completed exactly as expected.

The full routine should be realistic within the requested Service duration.

## 3. Medication Review

Where medication, supplements, or medical care are involved:

- [ ] Review each medication or supplement.
- [ ] Confirm dose.
- [ ] Confirm route.
- [ ] Confirm timing and acceptable timing window.
- [ ] Confirm storage location.
- [ ] Confirm administration method.
- [ ] Observe or review a demonstration where appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm what counts as successful administration.
- [ ] Confirm instructions if the pet refuses medication.
- [ ] Review known reactions, cautions, or handling concerns.
- [ ] Confirm prescribing veterinarian information where relevant.

Any medication need that is advanced, high-risk, exact-time, unusually time-intensive, requires significant restraint, or otherwise falls outside routine scope must be referred to the Medication Acceptance / Scope Review before booking approval.

## 4. Observe Normal Pet Handling

- [ ] Observe how the pet responds to the Client.
- [ ] Observe how the pet responds to Cuddle Crew where reasonably possible.
- [ ] Review normal handling preferences.
- [ ] Review touch sensitivities or areas to avoid.
- [ ] Review fear, hiding, or stranger-response patterns.
- [ ] Review expected greeting and entry behavior.
- [ ] Confirm any safe handling techniques already used by the Client.

The Meet & Greet is not a guarantee that future behavior will be identical, but obvious behavior or handling concerns should be documented and reviewed before Service.

## 5. Walking Equipment and Outdoor Handling

Where walking is part of the requested Service:

- [ ] Inspect and test the normal leash.
- [ ] Inspect and test harness, collar, or other walking equipment.
- [ ] Confirm the equipment fits securely.
- [ ] Review how the pet is normally leashed and unleashed.
- [ ] Review door, gate, elevator, stair, driveway, or other transition risks.
- [ ] Observe normal leash response where reasonably appropriate.
- [ ] Review pulling, reactivity, freezing, escape attempts, or other walking concerns.
- [ ] Confirm any Client-approved walking restrictions.

A dog that cannot safely be leashed or handled should not be forced into a walk.

Where safe walking is not workable, the requested Service may require modification, alternative care, personalized review, referral, or decline.

## 6. Behavior and Safety Review

Review and document where applicable:

- [ ] Bite history.
- [ ] Snap history.
- [ ] Resource guarding.
- [ ] Leash reactivity.
- [ ] Dog-to-dog or pet-to-pet conflict.
- [ ] Door-dashing or escape history.
- [ ] Stranger fear or defensive behavior.
- [ ] Handling sensitivity.
- [ ] Separation requirements.
- [ ] Predator/prey separation.
- [ ] Equipment-related safety concerns.
- [ ] Other known triggers.

Any materially elevated behavior or safety concern should be referred to the appropriate internal Behavior Risk Review before final booking acceptance.

Do not treat a completed Meet & Greet as automatic approval if a serious safety concern remains unresolved.

## 7. Access Testing

Test the actual access pathway expected to be used for Service.

- [ ] Primary door or entry method tested.
- [ ] Physical key tested where applicable.
- [ ] Lockbox tested where applicable.
- [ ] Keypad or smart-lock code tested where applicable.
- [ ] Gate access tested.
- [ ] Garage access reviewed where relevant.
- [ ] Building, concierge, or leasing-office restrictions reviewed.
- [ ] Alarm procedure tested or clearly reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] Backup access method confirmed.
- [ ] Parking or building-entry requirements confirmed.

Do not assume that a submitted key, code, or written access instruction works until it has been tested where reasonably possible.

If access cannot be confirmed, the booking should remain pending until a workable access plan is established.

## 8. Locate Pet and Home Supplies

Confirm the location of applicable supplies:

- [ ] Pet food.
- [ ] Treats.
- [ ] Water supplies.
- [ ] Medication.
- [ ] Leashes, collars, harnesses, and walking equipment.
- [ ] Waste bags.
- [ ] Litter and litter supplies.
- [ ] Enclosure-cleaning supplies.
- [ ] Pet cleaning supplies.
- [ ] Towels.
- [ ] Carriers.
- [ ] Emergency pet equipment.
- [ ] Cleaning supplies for routine agreed pet messes.
- [ ] Other specialty care supplies.

Identify any supply issue that must be resolved before Service.

## 9. Emergency and Veterinary Information

- [ ] Confirm primary veterinarian.
- [ ] Confirm veterinary phone number.
- [ ] Confirm preferred emergency veterinary hospital where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm emergency contact hierarchy.
- [ ] Confirm pet carriers or emergency equipment locations.
- [ ] Confirm any relevant emergency instructions.
- [ ] Confirm required Emergency Veterinary Authorization status.

Do not infer emergency spending authority or medical authority from verbal conversation alone where a signed authorization is required.

## 10. Cameras, Audio Devices, and Privacy

- [ ] Review disclosed indoor cameras.
- [ ] Review disclosed outdoor cameras.
- [ ] Review audio-capable devices where relevant.
- [ ] Confirm any areas intentionally not monitored.
- [ ] For Overnight Care, review privacy expectations for sleeping, changing, bathing, and other reasonable private activity.

Camera disclosure should be handled consistently with the applicable Access, Key & Home Security Agreement and Overnight Care Addendum.

## 11. Home and Property Tasks

Where the Client requests incidental home-related tasks:

- [ ] Confirm mail instructions if agreed.
- [ ] Confirm package instructions if agreed.
- [ ] Confirm trash instructions if agreed.
- [ ] Confirm plant care if agreed.
- [ ] Confirm thermostat or HVAC instructions if relevant.
- [ ] Confirm blinds, lights, or home-security tasks if agreed.
- [ ] Confirm any other incidental home tasks.

Only agreed tasks that fit within Service scope and booked duration should be accepted.

The Meet & Greet should not silently expand pet-care Service into unlimited household management.

## 12. Service Duration Fit

- [ ] Review the complete requested care routine.
- [ ] Confirm that the selected Service duration realistically fits the routine.
- [ ] Confirm that pet care, walking, medication, cleanup, enrichment, home tasks, and required documentation can reasonably fit.
- [ ] Identify whether 30 minutes is adequate.
- [ ] Identify whether 60 minutes is more appropriate.
- [ ] Identify whether 90 minutes is more appropriate.
- [ ] Identify whether the care plan requires multiple Services or a different Service type.
- [ ] Identify whether personalized review is required.

Do not approve a shorter duration merely because it is less expensive if the complete care routine cannot realistically fit.

Care that may not fit within the standard maximum daytime duration should receive personalized review.

## 13. Scheduling and Arrival Expectations

- [ ] Review applicable Service window.
- [ ] Confirm that routine daytime Services are scheduled within arrival windows rather than exact-minute guarantees.
- [ ] Identify any exact-time requirement.
- [ ] Refer any exact-time care need for review before acceptance.
- [ ] Confirm known timing-sensitive feeding, medication, elimination, or care needs.
- [ ] Confirm any scheduling constraint that materially affects feasibility.

Routine daytime scheduling should remain consistent with current approved scheduling rules.

## 14. Pricing, Travel, and Modifiers

- [ ] Confirm selected Service and duration.
- [ ] Confirm additional-pet pricing where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm travel zone.
- [ ] Confirm any travel fee.
- [ ] Confirm holiday or peak-date treatment where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm short-notice or same-day modifier where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm any separate access-trip considerations where relevant.
- [ ] Confirm whether personalized/custom pricing review is required.
- [ ] Review applicable cancellation expectations with the Client.

The Meet & Greet does not independently create or change pricing rules.

Current approved Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy controls rates and modifiers.

Current approved Cancellation, Booking Change & Refund Policy controls cancellation treatment.

## 15. Travel and Route Feasibility

- [ ] Confirm the service location has been assigned the appropriate travel classification.
- [ ] Confirm the route is operationally workable.
- [ ] Consider applicable drive time and scheduling impact.
- [ ] Identify any extended-area issue requiring review.
- [ ] Confirm that travel feasibility is acceptable before booking approval.

Travel classification should be based on ordinary or typical one-way travel time under normal driving conditions rather than temporary traffic conditions.

A travel fee does not guarantee booking acceptance.

## 16. Confirm Client Questions and Expectations

- [ ] Ask whether the Client has remaining questions.
- [ ] Clarify Service scope.
- [ ] Clarify scheduling expectations.
- [ ] Clarify journal/update expectations.
- [ ] Clarify how booking requests are confirmed.
- [ ] Clarify that a quote or estimate does not by itself reserve calendar space.
- [ ] Clarify relevant policy questions.
- [ ] Clarify any special care concern that remains uncertain.

Do not make verbal promises that contradict the current approved Client-facing agreements or policies.

## 17. Identify Follow-Up Requirements

Before leaving the Meet & Greet, identify any unresolved item.

Potential follow-up may include:

- missing pet profile information;
- incomplete intake information;
- medication documentation;
- veterinary information;
- emergency authorization;
- behavior review;
- access correction;
- replacement walking equipment;
- service-duration change;
- schedule modification;
- pricing update;
- travel review;
- shared-care information;
- Overnight review;
- care-frequency adjustment;
- backup-provider authorization;
- or another unresolved onboarding issue.

### Follow-Up Required

Record internally:

- issue;
- responsible party;
- required action;
- target completion date where useful;
- and whether the issue blocks booking confirmation or only blocks first Service.

## 18. Meet & Greet Outcome

Select one:

- [ ] **ACCEPT — Meet & Greet complete and booking may proceed**
- [ ] **ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS**
- [ ] **PENDING FOLLOW-UP**
- [ ] **PERSONALIZED REVIEW REQUIRED**
- [ ] **MODIFY SERVICE PLAN**
- [ ] **REFER / OUT OF SCOPE**
- [ ] **DECLINE**

### Conditions / Follow-Up

Record any conditions required before Service begins.

### Notes

Record concise internal notes needed for safe and reliable care.

Do not record unnecessary personal observations unrelated to Service.

## Meet & Greet Completion Rule

Completion of the Meet & Greet does not automatically mean the booking is approved.

Booking acceptance still depends on the applicable:

- safety review;
- service and insurance scope;
- care-frequency review;
- duration fit;
- behavior review;
- medication review;
- access readiness;
- scheduling capacity;
- travel feasibility;
- agreements and authorizations;
- payment requirements;
- and other applicable pre-service requirements.

Where a material issue remains unresolved, the appropriate outcome is **Pending**, **Personalized review required**, **Modify**, **Refer**, or **Decline** rather than automatic acceptance.

## Website Implementation Notes

The website may explain the purpose and general expectations of the Meet & Greet, but should not publish the internal checklist in full.

### Public Meet & Greet Description

The website may safely explain that the complimentary initial Meet & Greet is generally used to:

- meet the Client and pets;
- review care routines;
- review medications where applicable;
- review behavior and handling needs;
- inspect walking equipment;
- review home and access instructions;
- test the planned access method;
- confirm emergency information;
- review scheduling expectations;
- make sure the selected Service duration fits;
- and answer final questions before care begins.

The public explanation should remain welcoming and practical rather than presenting the meeting as a formal risk inspection.

### Complimentary Status

The initial standard Meet & Greet is complimentary and generally about 30 minutes.

Do not create an automatic Meet & Greet charge unless a future CURRENT / APPROVED pricing source explicitly changes this rule.

Do not imply that a prospective Client is entitled to unlimited free rescheduling of missed onboarding appointments.

### Scheduling

The website may allow a Client to request or schedule a Meet & Greet where technically supported.

A scheduled Meet & Greet should remain distinct from:

- a confirmed pet-care booking;
- a guaranteed Service reservation;
- or final onboarding approval.

### Onboarding Progress

The website may show a high-level status such as:

- Meet & Greet not scheduled
- Meet & Greet scheduled
- Meet & Greet completed
- Follow-up needed
- Under review

Do not expose:

- internal risk findings;
- behavior risk scores;
- safety concerns;
- decline criteria;
- route burden;
- internal capacity calculations;
- or private sitter notes.

### Access Testing

A website form may collect preliminary access information, but the system must not mark access as successfully tested merely because the Client submitted a code or instructions.

Where useful, distinguish internally between:

- access information received;
- testing pending;
- tested successfully;
- issue requiring follow-up.

Sensitive access credentials must remain in the approved secure operational system.

### Behavior Information

The website may ask Clients to disclose relevant behavior history.

Client-facing language should focus on safety and accurate care planning.

Do not display internal risk classifications or internal acceptance thresholds.

### Equipment

The website may remind Clients to have normal walking and pet-care equipment available during the Meet & Greet.

Do not imply that Cuddle Crew supplies specialized Client-specific walking equipment unless an authoritative source expressly says so.

### Service-Duration Fit

The website may explain that the Meet & Greet helps confirm whether the requested duration realistically fits the pet’s routine.

The system must not automatically force a booking into the Client’s initially selected duration if the actual care routine requires more time.

Where the routine does not fit, appropriate outcomes may include:

- recommend a longer Service;
- recommend additional Services;
- modify the care plan;
- or require personalized review.

### Pricing

The website may explain that final scope and pricing are confirmed during the booking process.

Do not allow Meet & Greet-specific logic to create rates or modifiers that differ from the CURRENT / APPROVED Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy.

### Precise Petcare Handoff

Any pet, care, medication, access, or scheduling information confirmed during the Meet & Greet should ultimately be reflected in the appropriate Precise Petcare record.

The website must not become a competing authoritative care-instruction database.

### Follow-Up

If unresolved items remain after the Meet & Greet, the website or portal may show a general client-facing status such as:

**Action needed**

or:

**Additional information needed before your booking can be finalized**

Do not expose private internal reasons when doing so.

### Booking Acceptance

Do not display a definitive booking-accepted state merely because the Meet & Greet is complete.

The booking may still require:

- forms;
- authorizations;
- review;
- payment;
- schedule confirmation;
- or other pre-service requirements.

### Conflict Handling

If existing website behavior conflicts with this CURRENT / APPROVED reference:

1. flag the conflict;
2. identify the affected feature or content;
3. preserve the internal Meet & Greet rule;
4. do not silently simplify or eliminate a required check.

If this document conflicts with a CURRENT / APPROVED Client-facing agreement or policy, the Client-facing agreement or policy controls the contractual subject and the conflict should be flagged for human review.
