## Purpose

This document defines the internal suitability and acceptance review for Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC Overnight Care bookings.

It is used to determine whether a requested Overnight booking fits the standard Overnight Service structure and whether the home, pet-care needs, access, privacy, communication, travel, schedule, and continuity requirements are workable.

This document is an internal operational reference.

The public website may explain the basic Overnight framework and general home requirements, but it should not expose the full internal acceptance rubric, route/capacity considerations, privacy review notes, or decline reasoning.

Precise Petcare should remain the operational source of truth for current Client, pet, care, access, schedule, and confirmed booking records.

## Authoritative Content

# OVERNIGHT ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST

Before accepting an Overnight booking, review each applicable category below.

## 1. Standard Overnight Scope

- [ ] Requested Service fits the standard Overnight framework.
- [ ] Client understands that standard Overnight Care generally covers approximately 6:00 PM through 8:00 AM.
- [ ] Client understands that standard Overnight Care is not twenty-four-hour or continuous presence.
- [ ] Requested tasks are compatible with the Overnight Service scope.
- [ ] The booking does not require continuous daytime presence unless a separate Service or custom arrangement has been specifically reviewed and approved.

If the Client expects uninterrupted 24-hour presence, constant supervision, or another scope materially beyond the standard Overnight structure, the request requires personalized review.

## 2. Daytime Care Needs

- [ ] Daytime needs have been identified separately from the Overnight.
- [ ] Midday potty, feeding, medication, welfare, walking, or other daytime needs have been reviewed.
- [ ] Any separate daytime Service has been included in the care plan where needed.
- [ ] The Client understands that daytime care outside the Overnight period is not automatically included.

A standard midday add-on during an Overnight is one separate 30-minute daytime care visit.

Longer midday care should use the applicable standard 60- or 90-minute daytime Service.

A midday dog walk should use the applicable Dog Walk Service.

## 3. Care-Frequency Adequacy

- [ ] The complete Overnight + daytime care plan provides adequate welfare coverage for the pets.
- [ ] Feeding, water, elimination, medication, monitoring, and companionship needs are reasonably covered.
- [ ] The care plan does not rely on the Overnight alone where daytime welfare needs require additional care.
- [ ] Any prolonged gap without responsible in-person care has been reviewed.

An Overnight booking should not be accepted merely because the Overnight itself fits if the overall care-frequency plan is inadequate.

## 4. Sleeping Area

- [ ] A safe and reasonably sanitary sleeping area is available.
- [ ] Clean bedding or an appropriate sleeping arrangement is available.
- [ ] The sleeping area is reasonably private.
- [ ] The sleeping area does not create an obvious safety concern.
- [ ] Any household restrictions affecting the sleeping area have been disclosed.

The requested sleeping arrangement should be workable for a professional sitter.

Cuddle Crew is not required to accept an unsafe, unsanitary, or unreasonable sleeping arrangement.

## 5. Bathroom and Basic Utilities

- [ ] Functioning bathroom access is available.
- [ ] Running water is available.
- [ ] Electricity is available.
- [ ] Appropriate heating and/or cooling is available.
- [ ] Basic home conditions are reasonably suitable for an overnight stay.

If essential utilities are unavailable or unreliable, the booking may require modification, postponement, or decline.

## 6. HVAC and Environmental Conditions

- [ ] Home temperature can be maintained at a reasonably safe level.
- [ ] HVAC instructions are available where relevant.
- [ ] Pet-specific temperature requirements are understood.
- [ ] Known environmental hazards have been disclosed.
- [ ] The home does not present an obvious overnight health or safety problem.

The sitter should not be expected to remain overnight in materially unsafe heat, cold, smoke, contamination, or other unsafe environmental conditions.

## 7. Household Occupants and Visitors

- [ ] All expected household occupants have been disclosed.
- [ ] Expected overnight visitors have been disclosed.
- [ ] Expected service providers or third parties have been disclosed where relevant.
- [ ] Shared access expectations are understood.
- [ ] The presence of another person does not create an unresolved safety, privacy, or responsibility issue.

Unexpected or undisclosed persons in the home may require review or may justify leaving or declining entry if the situation reasonably appears unsafe.

## 8. Cameras, Audio Devices, and Privacy

- [ ] Relevant indoor cameras have been disclosed.
- [ ] Relevant outdoor cameras have been disclosed.
- [ ] Audio-capable devices have been disclosed where relevant.
- [ ] Sleeping-area privacy has been reviewed.
- [ ] Changing and bathing privacy is reasonably protected.
- [ ] No camera/audio arrangement creates an unreasonable privacy concern for the Overnight Service.

Cameras or audio devices should not be placed where a sitter reasonably expects personal privacy during sleeping, changing, bathing, or similar private activity.

If the surveillance setup is incompatible with reasonable overnight privacy, the booking should be modified or declined.

## 9. Access and After-Dark Entry

- [ ] Primary access method is workable.
- [ ] Backup access method is available where appropriate.
- [ ] Locks, keys, keypad, smart-lock, gate, garage, concierge, or building-entry requirements are understood.
- [ ] Access has been tested where reasonably possible.
- [ ] After-dark entry and exit are reasonably safe.
- [ ] Parking is workable.
- [ ] Lighting or other entry conditions do not create an unresolved safety concern.

An Overnight booking should not be accepted with unresolved access problems.

## 10. Parking

- [ ] Overnight parking is permitted where required.
- [ ] Parking restrictions are understood.
- [ ] Required permits or guest-parking instructions are available.
- [ ] Parking does not create an unreasonable safety or access burden.
- [ ] Any unusual parking cost or limitation has been identified.

Parking feasibility should be considered before confirmation, especially for apartments, gated communities, urban areas, or locations with restricted overnight parking.

## 11. Wi-Fi and Cellular Communication

- [ ] Reliable Wi-Fi or cellular service is available for required business communication.

**OR**

- [ ] An alternative communication plan has been specifically agreed.

Cuddle Crew needs a workable method to:

- send Client updates;
- access necessary business systems;
- contact the Client;
- contact emergency contacts;
- and respond to emergencies.

A weak-signal location is not automatically unacceptable, but the communication plan must be workable.

## 12. Home Emergency Contact

- [ ] A home/property emergency contact is available where appropriate.
- [ ] Contact information is current.
- [ ] The person’s role is understood.
- [ ] The contact is appropriate for issues such as plumbing, electrical, HVAC, security, or other property emergencies where relevant.

This person is separate from veterinary or pet-care emergency contacts unless the same individual serves both roles.

## 13. Pet Behavior Overnight

- [ ] Pet behavior is reasonably workable for Overnight Care.
- [ ] Stranger response is understood.
- [ ] Nighttime behavior is understood where relevant.
- [ ] Sleep-location expectations for pets are understood.
- [ ] Resource guarding concerns are understood.
- [ ] Escape or door-dashing risk is understood.
- [ ] Pet-to-pet separation requirements are workable.
- [ ] Predator/prey separation is workable where applicable.
- [ ] The sitter can safely move through the home at night.

Behavior concerns that are materially elevated require appropriate Behavior Risk Review before acceptance.

## 14. Medication Overnight

- [ ] Overnight medication needs are documented.
- [ ] Timing is workable.
- [ ] Administration method is within current scope.
- [ ] Exact-time or high-risk medication has been separately reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] Medication can be completed safely without forceful restraint.
- [ ] Daytime medication needs outside the Overnight period have been accounted for separately.

Advanced, high-risk, exact-time, restraint-intensive, or otherwise complex medication may require personalized review.

## 15. Escape and Security Risk

- [ ] Exterior doors are secure.
- [ ] Gates are secure where relevant.
- [ ] Windows or other pet escape routes are reasonably understood.
- [ ] Crate, room, enclosure, or confinement routines are workable.
- [ ] Known door-dashing or escape history has been reviewed.
- [ ] The nighttime security plan is workable.

Materially elevated escape risk may require:

- additional conditions;
- modified care;
- equipment changes;
- behavior review;
- or decline.

## 16. Pet Supplies

- [ ] Adequate food is available.
- [ ] Adequate medication is available.
- [ ] Adequate litter/enclosure supplies are available.
- [ ] Necessary cleaning supplies are available.
- [ ] Required walking equipment is available.
- [ ] Pet carriers or emergency equipment are accessible where applicable.
- [ ] Supplies are sufficient for the full booking period.

Insufficient essential supplies should be addressed before the booking begins where reasonably possible.

## 17. Home Tasks and Scope Creep

- [ ] Agreed incidental home tasks are documented.
- [ ] Tasks reasonably fit within Overnight scope.
- [ ] Mail, plants, trash, lights, blinds, packages, or similar requests are clearly defined where applicable.
- [ ] The requested household work does not materially transform the booking into a different service.

Overnight Care does not include unlimited:

- housekeeping;
- household management;
- errands;
- property maintenance;
- or unrelated personal-assistant work.

Material additional scope requires separate review.

## 18. Travel Zone

- [ ] Applicable travel zone has been confirmed.
- [ ] Travel burden is workable.
- [ ] Overnight-specific travel review has been completed where required.

Standard Overnight pricing automatically applies within the normal approved area.

Overnight Services outside the Standard Zone require individualized review.

Do not automatically apply the daytime Extended or Far Extended travel surcharge to an Overnight booking.

A daytime travel fee structure does not itself determine Overnight acceptance.

## 19. Route and Schedule Impact

- [ ] Overnight location is compatible with surrounding confirmed Client obligations.
- [ ] Evening arrival is workable.
- [ ] Morning departure is workable.
- [ ] Any separate midday or daytime Services are workable.
- [ ] Travel between the Overnight location and other Client Services is sustainable.
- [ ] Personal/work/school schedule impact has been considered where relevant.
- [ ] The booking will not create unreliable care for other confirmed Clients.

Do not expose internal route or scheduling calculations publicly.

## 20. Safety of the Location

- [ ] General location is reasonably safe for arrival and departure.
- [ ] After-dark access is workable.
- [ ] Building, parking, gate, or surrounding-area conditions do not create an unresolved safety concern.
- [ ] Known household hazards have been disclosed.
- [ ] The sitter can safely leave if necessary.

Cuddle Crew may decline or discontinue Overnight Care if conditions reasonably appear unsafe.

## 21. Shared Care / Third Parties

If another caregiver or person will provide care during the Overnight booking:

- [ ] Other caregiver has been disclosed.
- [ ] Responsibilities are clearly assigned.
- [ ] Medication responsibility is clearly assigned.
- [ ] Access responsibility is clear.
- [ ] Daytime responsibilities are clear.
- [ ] Emergency responsibilities are clear.
- [ ] Communication plan is workable.

A vague plan that another person "might stop by" is not treated as a reliable care plan.

Complex shared-care arrangements require review.

## 22. Client Availability and Emergency Contacts

- [ ] Client contact method is confirmed.
- [ ] Client travel/time-zone considerations are understood where relevant.
- [ ] Emergency contacts are current.
- [ ] Veterinary contact information is current.
- [ ] Emergency Veterinary Authorization is complete where required.
- [ ] Home/property emergency contact is available where appropriate.

The care plan should remain workable even if the Client cannot immediately respond.

## 23. Backup / Continuity Planning

- [ ] Client’s backup-provider authorization or decline is recorded where applicable.
- [ ] Applicable continuity pathway is understood.
- [ ] Access/privacy considerations for any approved backup provider are workable.
- [ ] The booking does not depend on an unapproved person taking over.

Only approved continuity or backup pathways may be used.

Do not expose private backup-provider details publicly.

## 24. Long-Stay Trigger

If the booking consists of seven (7) or more consecutive Overnights:

- [ ] Long-Stay Review has been completed.

Seven or more consecutive Overnights trigger an internal capacity and sustainability review.

This is a review trigger, not an automatic decline and not an automatic long-stay surcharge.

The Long-Stay Review should consider:

- personal/work/school calendar;
- daytime care obligations;
- supplies;
- scope creep;
- route sustainability;
- other Clients;
- emergency contacts;
- continuity;
- and overall sustainability.

## 25. Holiday / Peak-Date Review

- [ ] Booking dates have been checked against the current approved Holiday / Peak-Date Calendar.
- [ ] Applicable Overnight holiday surcharge has been identified where relevant.
- [ ] Applicable holiday cancellation rules have been disclosed where relevant.
- [ ] Holiday capacity is available.

Holiday pricing does not guarantee availability.

## 26. Short-Notice Review

- [ ] Determine whether the Overnight was requested less than forty-eight (48) hours before the requested Service.
- [ ] Determine whether the applicable short-notice fee applies.
- [ ] Confirm that the booking can still be safely and operationally accommodated.

Payment of a short-notice fee does not require Cuddle Crew to accept the booking.

## 27. Pricing and Payment

- [ ] Overnight base price confirmed.
- [ ] Additional-pet pricing confirmed where applicable.
- [ ] Separate daytime/midday Services confirmed where applicable.
- [ ] Holiday treatment confirmed where applicable.
- [ ] Short-notice treatment confirmed where applicable.
- [ ] Any custom pricing disclosed before confirmation.
- [ ] Any reservation-payment requirement disclosed.
- [ ] Applicable payment deadline established.

Current Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy controls rates, fees, reservation payments, and payment timing.

Payment does not override:

- home suitability;
- safety;
- behavior;
- medication scope;
- travel feasibility;
- scheduling capacity;
- privacy;
- or other acceptance requirements.

## 28. Overnight Acceptance Decision

Select one:

- [ ] **ACCEPT**
- [ ] **ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS**
- [ ] **MODIFY OVERNIGHT PLAN**
- [ ] **PERSONALIZED REVIEW REQUIRED**
- [ ] **RECOMMEND DAYTIME CARE INSTEAD**
- [ ] **REFER**
- [ ] **DECLINE**
- [ ] **PENDING**

### Conditions / Required Changes

Record internally any applicable conditions, such as:

- separate midday visit;
- longer daytime visit;
- medication condition;
- access correction;
- parking arrangement;
- camera/privacy adjustment;
- equipment requirement;
- behavior-management condition;
- schedule condition;
- continuity requirement;
- or other necessary modification.

### Notes

Record only information reasonably necessary for:

- safe care;
- service planning;
- continuity;
- access;
- scheduling;
- scope;
- or future booking consistency.

Do not record unnecessary personal commentary about the Client or home.

## 29. Overnight Acceptance Principle

An Overnight booking should be accepted only when:

- the requested scope fits the Overnight Service structure;
- the overall care-frequency plan is adequate;
- the home is reasonably suitable for an overnight stay;
- access is safe and reliable;
- reasonable sitter privacy is protected;
- required communication is workable;
- pet behavior and medication needs are manageable;
- travel and scheduling impact are workable;
- continuity requirements are addressed;
- and all applicable onboarding, authorization, pricing, and payment requirements are satisfied.

A higher price does not make an unsafe, unworkable, uninsurable, or out-of-scope Overnight acceptable.

Where the standard Overnight structure does not fit, use modification or personalized review rather than forcing the booking into the standard Service.

## Website Implementation Notes

The website may explain the general Overnight framework and collect preliminary information, but the full internal acceptance checklist must not be publicly exposed.

### Public Overnight Description

The website may explain that standard Overnight Care:

- generally runs approximately 6:00 PM–8:00 AM;
- provides evening-through-morning in-home care;
- does not include 24-hour continuous presence;
- and may be paired with a separate daytime visit or walk where needed.

Avoid ambiguous phrases such as:

- "full-day care";
- "24-hour pet sitting";
- "around-the-clock care";
- or "continuous care"

unless a separate Service explicitly provides that scope.

### Midday Care

The website should clearly distinguish:

- standard Overnight;
- Overnight + one 30-minute midday care visit;
- longer midday care;
- and midday Dog Walks.

Do not present the standard midday add-on as unlimited daytime care.

### Preliminary Overnight Suitability Questions

The website may collect high-level information such as:

- pets and species;
- requested dates;
- daytime care needs;
- medication needs;
- behavior considerations;
- access type;
- general parking/building situation;
- household occupants;
- cameras/audio devices;
- Wi-Fi/cellular availability;
- and whether the stay is seven or more nights.

Responses may trigger review but must not automatically expose the internal reason.

### Home Suitability

The public website may state generally that Overnight Care requires a safe, reasonably sanitary home with:

- appropriate sleeping arrangements;
- bathroom access;
- running water;
- electricity;
- and safe heating/cooling.

Avoid publishing unnecessarily detailed internal inspection criteria.

### Privacy

The website may state that disclosed cameras and audio devices are permitted subject to reasonable sitter privacy.

It may explain that sleeping, changing, and bathing areas must provide reasonable privacy.

Do not expose internal privacy assessment notes.

### Access

Website forms may collect preliminary access information.

Submitting access instructions does not mean access has been successfully tested.

Sensitive keys, codes, alarm details, and other access information should remain in the approved secure operational system.

### Communication

The website may explain that reliable communication access is needed during Overnight Care.

Do not promise that Wi-Fi is strictly mandatory in every circumstance if an alternative communication plan may be approved.

### Behavior and Medication

The website may ask Clients to disclose relevant behavior or medication information.

Do not create a public risk score or approval score.

Complex cases should route to:

**Personalized review required**

### Travel

Do not automatically quote standard Overnight pricing for locations outside the Standard Zone.

Where the overnight location requires extended travel review, stop automatic finalization and require human review.

Do not expose the private service-area reference point.

### Long-Stay Logic

A booking of seven or more consecutive Overnights should trigger internal Long-Stay Review.

Public UX may say:

**Extended stays require a quick personalized review before confirmation.**

Do not state or imply that seven nights automatically means:

- decline;
- surcharge;
- or acceptance.

### Availability and Capacity

Do not treat an open calendar date as automatic Overnight availability.

Internal review may consider:

- other confirmed Clients;
- midday obligations;
- morning/evening routing;
- personal calendar;
- travel;
- and continuity.

Do not expose those calculations publicly.

### Pricing

The website must derive Overnight rates and modifiers from the CURRENT / APPROVED Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy.

This document does not create:

- new travel fees;
- long-stay surcharges;
- privacy fees;
- behavior fees;
- medication-risk fees;
- or home-condition fees.

### Booking Status

Recommended public states include:

- Request received
- Additional information needed
- Under review
- Personalized review required
- Booking confirmed
- Unable to accommodate

Do not expose internal labels or private reasoning.

### Precise Petcare Handoff

Final Client-specific Overnight information should be maintained in Precise Petcare, including:

- pet routines;
- daytime care plan;
- access details;
- medication information;
- household occupants;
- camera disclosures;
- home notes;
- emergency contacts;
- and confirmed booking scope.

The website should not become a second authoritative Overnight care-plan database.

### Conflict Handling

If website behavior conflicts with this CURRENT / APPROVED Overnight Acceptance reference:

3. preserve the authoritative internal rule;

If this document conflicts with a CURRENT / APPROVED Client-facing agreement or policy, the Client-facing document controls the contractual subject and the conflict should be flagged for human review.
