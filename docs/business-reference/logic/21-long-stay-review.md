## Purpose

This document defines the internal review required for Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC bookings consisting of seven (7) or more consecutive Overnight Services.

The purpose of the Long-Stay Review is to determine whether the full booking remains safe, sustainable, operationally workable, and compatible with the pets’ needs, the Client’s expectations, Cuddle Crew’s schedule, route obligations, supplies, emergency planning, and other confirmed commitments.

Seven or more consecutive Overnights trigger this review because a longer booking reserves a substantial block of time and can create cumulative scheduling, route, supply, continuity, and workload considerations that may not be apparent when reviewing a single Overnight.

This is a review trigger only.

A Long-Stay Review does **not** automatically create:

- a long-stay surcharge;
- a long-stay discount;
- a multi-day discount;
- automatic acceptance;
- or automatic decline.

This document is an internal operational reference. The public website may state that extended stays require personalized review, but it should not expose the full internal sustainability analysis.

Precise Petcare should remain the operational source of truth for current Client, pet, care, access, schedule, and confirmed booking records.

## Authoritative Content

# LONG-STAY REVIEW CHECKLIST

Complete this review for any booking consisting of seven (7) or more consecutive Overnight Services.

## 1. Booking Dates and Length

Record internally:

- first Overnight date;
- final Overnight date;
- total number of consecutive nights;
- any separate daytime Services;
- any midday visits;
- any Dog Walks;
- any holiday or peak-date overlap;
- and any known travel or schedule complications.

- [ ] Total number of consecutive Overnights has been confirmed.
- [ ] Full booking dates have been reviewed.
- [ ] All associated daytime care has been identified.

## 2. Personal / Work / School Calendar

- [ ] Personal calendar has been reviewed for the entire stay.
- [ ] Work obligations have been reviewed.
- [ ] School obligations have been reviewed.
- [ ] Existing appointments or fixed commitments have been reviewed.
- [ ] Morning and evening timing remains workable.
- [ ] Required sleep and recovery remain realistic.
- [ ] There is no known conflict that would make reliable care impractical.

A long booking should not be accepted merely because the individual Overnight dates appear open.

The full duration should be considered as one sustained commitment.

## 3. Midday and Daytime Care Needs

- [ ] All daytime care needs have been identified.
- [ ] Midday visit frequency is appropriate.
- [ ] Any required Dog Walks have been identified.
- [ ] Medication outside the Overnight window has been identified.
- [ ] Feeding or elimination needs outside the Overnight window have been identified.
- [ ] Daytime care can be completed reliably for the full stay.

Standard Overnight Care generally covers approximately 6:00 PM through 8:00 AM and does not include unlimited daytime care.

A standard midday add-on is one separate 30-minute daytime care visit.

Longer midday care uses the applicable standard 60- or 90-minute daytime rate.

A midday dog walk uses the applicable Dog Walk rate.

## 4. Overall Care-Frequency Sustainability

- [ ] The combined Overnight and daytime care plan is adequate for the pets’ welfare.
- [ ] The plan remains adequate for every day of the booking, not only the first few days.
- [ ] Feeding, water, elimination, medication, companionship, exercise, and monitoring needs remain appropriately covered.
- [ ] No portion of the plan depends on an unrealistic or unreliable assumption.

If the requested care plan becomes inadequate over a longer absence, require an adjusted plan before confirming the booking.

## 5. Food and Consumable Supplies

Confirm sufficient quantities for the entire booking:

- [ ] Pet food.
- [ ] Treats where applicable.
- [ ] Litter.
- [ ] Hay or fresh food for small animals where applicable.
- [ ] Bedding or enclosure materials.
- [ ] Waste bags.
- [ ] Cleaning supplies.
- [ ] Other routine consumables.

A reasonable extra supply margin is preferable where practical.

Any foreseeable supply problem should be addressed before the stay begins.

## 6. Medication and Medical Supplies

- [ ] Medication quantity is sufficient for the full booking.
- [ ] Required supplements are sufficient.
- [ ] Medication storage is workable.
- [ ] Medication schedule remains workable for the entire stay.
- [ ] Exact-time requirements have already received appropriate review where applicable.
- [ ] Advanced or high-risk medical needs remain within approved scope.
- [ ] Refill or veterinary contingency is understood where relevant.

A medication plan that is workable for one or two days may still be unsustainable over a longer booking.

Any unresolved medical-scope concern should be reviewed before confirmation.

## 7. Pet Medical and Behavior Sustainability

- [ ] Known medical needs are sustainable for the full stay.
- [ ] Known behavior needs are sustainable for the full stay.
- [ ] Separation or rotation routines remain manageable.
- [ ] Escape-management requirements remain realistic.
- [ ] Required handling does not create cumulative unreasonable risk.
- [ ] The pet can reasonably tolerate the planned care arrangement for the full Client absence.

Consider whether a routine that is technically possible for one Overnight becomes materially more difficult when repeated for seven or more nights.

If medical, behavior, or handling requirements create cumulative risk or workload concerns, require additional review or modification.

## 8. Home Tasks and Scope Creep

Review all non-pet household tasks requested for the stay.

- [ ] Mail responsibilities are defined where applicable.
- [ ] Package responsibilities are defined where applicable.
- [ ] Trash responsibilities are defined where applicable.
- [ ] Plant care is defined where applicable.
- [ ] Lights, blinds, thermostat, or home-security tasks are defined where applicable.
- [ ] Other incidental household responsibilities are clearly limited.
- [ ] Tasks do not create excessive scope creep over the duration of the booking.

Small incidental tasks can become significant workload when repeated over a long stay.

Do not allow a long Overnight booking to silently expand into:

- extensive housekeeping;
- ongoing property management;
- large-scale plant care;
- repeated errands;
- maintenance work;
- or other unrelated responsibilities outside approved scope.

Material additional work should be separately reviewed.

## 9. Route Obligations

- [ ] Travel to and from the Overnight location remains workable.
- [ ] Morning route obligations are sustainable.
- [ ] Evening route obligations are sustainable.
- [ ] Midday/daytime route obligations are sustainable.
- [ ] The booking does not create unreasonable repeated backtracking.
- [ ] Other confirmed Clients can still be served reliably.
- [ ] Travel burden remains acceptable across the full booking.

Route feasibility must consider the whole booking period.

Do not accept a long booking solely because its price or travel surcharge can be calculated mathematically.

## 10. Other Clients and Existing Commitments

- [ ] Existing confirmed Client Services have been reviewed.
- [ ] The long stay does not require compressing care for other Clients.
- [ ] Arrival windows remain realistic.
- [ ] Required buffers remain available.
- [ ] Existing recurring Services remain sustainable.
- [ ] Holiday or high-demand capacity remains workable where applicable.

Confirmed care obligations should not be compromised merely to fit a long Overnight booking.

## 11. Capacity and Workload

Capacity should consider:

- Overnight commitment;
- daytime Service time;
- drive time;
- journal and documentation time;
- route transitions;
- personal schedule;
- realistic buffer;
- pet-specific needs;
- and other confirmed obligations.

- [ ] Full workload remains sustainable.
- [ ] Required documentation time is realistic.
- [ ] Appropriate buffer remains available.
- [ ] No time block is being artificially compressed.
- [ ] The booking does not depend on unrealistic perfect conditions every day.

Capacity is not based merely on the number of visits or apparent open calendar slots.

## 12. Emergency Contacts

- [ ] Client emergency contacts are current.
- [ ] Veterinary information is current.
- [ ] Preferred emergency veterinary facility is current where applicable.
- [ ] Home/property emergency contact is current where applicable.
- [ ] Emergency Veterinary Authorization is complete where required.
- [ ] Emergency spending or treatment instructions are documented where applicable.

Longer bookings increase the importance of having reliable emergency pathways before the Client leaves.

## 13. Backup and Continuity Path

- [ ] Client backup-provider authorization or decline is current where applicable.
- [ ] Approved backup or continuity pathway is current.
- [ ] Access-transfer method is understood where applicable.
- [ ] Privacy and information-sharing limits are understood.
- [ ] The booking does not depend on an unapproved substitute.
- [ ] There is a plan if Cuddle Crew becomes unexpectedly unable to complete care.

Only approved continuity pathways should be used.

Do not share keys, access codes, medical information, or Client security information with an unauthorized person.

## 14. Client Availability and Time Zone

- [ ] Client’s expected availability during the trip is understood.
- [ ] Client’s destination time zone is understood where relevant.
- [ ] Preferred communication method is current.
- [ ] Emergency contact pathway is workable if the Client is asleep, unavailable, traveling internationally, or otherwise difficult to reach.
- [ ] Any known periods of limited communication have been identified.

The booking should remain workable even when the Client cannot respond immediately.

## 15. Home Suitability Over the Full Stay

- [ ] Sleeping arrangement remains appropriate for the full booking.
- [ ] Bathroom access remains workable.
- [ ] Water and electricity are reliable.
- [ ] HVAC is workable.
- [ ] Parking remains workable.
- [ ] After-dark access remains workable.
- [ ] Wi-Fi/cellular communication remains workable or an alternative exists.
- [ ] Camera/privacy conditions remain acceptable.
- [ ] Household occupants or visitor arrangements remain clear.

A condition acceptable for one night may not necessarily be reasonable for an extended stay.

## 16. Household Occupants and Third Parties

- [ ] Expected occupants are disclosed.
- [ ] Expected visitors are disclosed.
- [ ] Other caregivers are disclosed.
- [ ] Shared-care responsibilities are clearly assigned.
- [ ] Access responsibilities are clear.
- [ ] Emergency responsibilities are clear.
- [ ] Medication responsibilities are clear where applicable.

Repeated interaction with third parties over a long stay may create additional access, privacy, responsibility, or communication complexity.

Unclear shared-care arrangements require review.

## 17. Client-Supplied Equipment

- [ ] Walking equipment is sufficient and functional.
- [ ] Carriers are available where relevant.
- [ ] Feeding equipment is functional.
- [ ] Automatic feeders or fountains are functioning where relied upon.
- [ ] Enclosures are secure.
- [ ] Required pet-care equipment is available.
- [ ] Backup equipment or alternative instructions exist where appropriate.

Automation or equipment may support the care plan but should not be treated as a substitute for necessary in-person welfare checks.

## 18. Booking Changes During the Stay

Consider whether likely foreseeable changes would be manageable, including:

- Client returning early;
- Client extending the trip;
- pet becoming temporarily unavailable;
- veterinary hospitalization;
- medication change;
- access failure;
- weather disruption;
- or another significant change.

- [ ] Client understands that booking changes remain subject to the current Cancellation, Booking Change & Refund Policy.
- [ ] Additional Services remain subject to availability.
- [ ] Trip extensions are not guaranteed.

A long stay should not be accepted on the assumption that unlimited extensions can always be accommodated.

## 19. Holiday and Peak-Date Impact

- [ ] Exact booking dates have been checked against the CURRENT / APPROVED Holiday / Peak-Date Calendar.
- [ ] Applicable holiday pricing has been identified.
- [ ] Applicable holiday cancellation rules have been identified.
- [ ] Peak-period schedule sustainability has been reviewed.

Holiday or peak pricing does not guarantee capacity.

## 20. Pricing Review

- [ ] Standard Overnight pricing has been applied correctly.
- [ ] Additional-pet pricing has been applied correctly where applicable.
- [ ] Daytime/midday pricing has been applied correctly.
- [ ] Holiday modifiers have been applied correctly where applicable.
- [ ] Short-notice modifiers have been applied correctly where applicable.
- [ ] Any custom scope has been reviewed.
- [ ] Final price is confirmed before booking acceptance.

Seven or more consecutive Overnights do **not** automatically create a special long-stay surcharge.

## 21. No Automatic Long-Stay Discount

- [ ] No automatic long-stay discount has been promised.

Current policy does not guarantee a:

- multi-day discount;
- weekly discount;
- package discount;
- repeat-client discount;
- or long-stay discount

merely because the booking includes seven or more consecutive Overnights.

A discretionary courtesy or custom price may be approved where appropriate, but it does not create a standard future discount.

## 22. No Automatic Long-Stay Surcharge

- [ ] No automatic long-stay surcharge has been applied merely because the booking is long.

Seven or more consecutive Overnights trigger an internal sustainability review, not an automatic surcharge.

Additional pricing may still arise from normal approved rules, such as:

- additional pets;
- separate daytime Services;
- holiday pricing;
- short-notice pricing;
- authorized direct costs;
- changed scope;
- or individualized custom pricing where the actual Service structure materially differs.

The length of the stay by itself does not create a special fee.

## 23. Reservation Payment and Payment Timing

- [ ] Any booking-specific reservation-payment requirement has been disclosed.
- [ ] Applicable payment deadline has been established.
- [ ] Any payment requirements due before confirmation have been satisfied.
- [ ] Any approved alternative payment arrangement is documented.

The size or duration of a long stay may justify a booking-specific reservation-payment arrangement where allowed by the current approved Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy and Cancellation, Booking Change & Refund Policy.

Do not invent a special deposit percentage merely because a booking is seven or more nights.

Payment does not override sustainability, safety, scope, or capacity concerns.

## 24. Final Sustainability Check

Before confirming the booking, ask:

- Can I reliably maintain this schedule for the entire stay?
- Can I continue meeting my obligations to other confirmed Clients?
- Is enough time available for travel, documentation, pet needs, and realistic buffer?
- Are the pet’s medical and behavior needs sustainable?
- Are supplies sufficient?
- Is the Client reachable through a workable emergency hierarchy?
- Is the home suitable for the duration?
- Is backup/continuity planning current?
- Is the full Service scope clearly defined?
- Am I relying on any unrealistic assumptions for the booking to work?

If any material answer is uncertain, resolve it before confirming the booking.

## 25. Long-Stay Decision

Select one:

- [ ] **APPROVE**
- [ ] **APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS**
- [ ] **MODIFY SERVICE PLAN**
- [ ] **PERSONALIZED REVIEW REQUIRED**
- [ ] **PENDING**
- [ ] **REFER**
- [ ] **DECLINE**

### Conditions / Required Changes

Record internally any applicable conditions, including:

- different daytime care frequency;
- Service-duration change;
- supply requirement;
- schedule modification;
- route condition;
- shared-care clarification;
- medication condition;
- emergency-contact update;
- backup-plan update;
- home-task limitation;
- or other necessary modification.

### Decision / Notes

Record:

- decision;
- applicable conditions;
- unresolved items;
- approval date;
- and any follow-up required before confirmation.

## 26. Long-Stay Principle

The purpose of Long-Stay Review is sustainability, not punishment or automatic pricing adjustment.

A seven-or-more-night booking should be approved only when the complete commitment remains reasonably workable across:

- Client care;
- pet welfare;
- medication;
- behavior;
- supplies;
- household tasks;
- route obligations;
- other Clients;
- personal schedule;
- emergency planning;
- continuity;
- and overall capacity.

If the standard plan is not sustainable, modify the plan or require personalized review.

Do not compress care, eliminate reasonable buffer, overbook the schedule, or create unrealistic arrival expectations solely to accept a long stay.
