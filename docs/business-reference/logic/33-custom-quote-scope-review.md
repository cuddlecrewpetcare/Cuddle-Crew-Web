> Status: CURRENT / APPROVED
>
> Classification: INTERNAL REFERENCE
>
> Authority: AUTHORITATIVE FOR THE INTERNAL CUSTOM-QUOTE AND SCOPE-REVIEW LOGIC COVERED BY THIS DOCUMENT
>
> Website use: Internal logic for bookings that should not be automatically quoted. Use to determine when the website should return “Personalized review required” rather than calculate a definitive price.

## Purpose

This document defines the internal Custom Quote / Scope Review process used by Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC when a requested booking does not clearly fit the standard automated pricing and Service structure.

A personalized quote or personal review is required when time, risk, scope, welfare, travel, complexity, or another material factor may change:

- the appropriate Service type;
- the required Service duration;
- the number or frequency of Services;
- the applicable pricing;
- whether the booking can be safely provided;
- whether the booking remains within current training, insurance, and Service scope;
- or whether the request should instead be modified, referred, or declined.

This document is an internal operational reference.

It is specifically intended to define when automation should stop.

The public website may identify that a request requires personalized review, but it should not expose the full internal trigger list, risk analysis, scope reasoning, route calculations, insurance analysis, or decline rationale.

Precise Petcare should remain the operational source of truth for final Client-specific booking scope, confirmed Services, approved pricing, and current care records.

## Authoritative Content

# CUSTOM QUOTE / SCOPE REVIEW

Complete this review whenever a requested booking triggers one or more conditions that make automatic quoting unreliable or inappropriate.

## 1. Client / Household

Record internally:

- Client / household;
- requested dates;
- requested Services;
- pets/species;
- requested duration;
- requested care frequency;
- requested travel/location;
- and any known unusual scope.

Do not begin by assigning a custom price.

First determine what Service plan is actually required and whether Cuddle Crew should provide it.

## 2. Automatic Review Triggers

Check every condition that applies.

- [ ] **4 or more dogs**
- [ ] **5 or more total pets**
- [ ] **3 or more materially distinct care types / routines**
- [ ] **Advanced or high-risk medication**
- [ ] **Exact-time care**
- [ ] **Significant behavior or safety concern**
- [ ] **Complex separation / predator-prey management**
- [ ] **Unusual species / husbandry**
- [ ] **Continuous / 24-hour care request**
- [ ] **Pet transportation request**
- [ ] **More than 45 minutes typical one-way travel**
- [ ] **Complex / extended-area Overnight**
- [ ] **Routine may not fit within 90 minutes**
- [ ] **Another material circumstance that does not fit the standard Service structure**

One or more checked items means:

**Stop automatic quoting and complete personalized review.**

These are review triggers, not automatic decline rules.

## 3. Large Dog Household

If the household includes 4 or more dogs:

- [ ] Complete routine has been reviewed.
- [ ] Feeding needs are understood.
- [ ] Walking requirements are understood.
- [ ] Separation requirements are understood.
- [ ] Behavior and handling are workable.
- [ ] Duration is realistic.
- [ ] Additional-pet pricing remains appropriate to the actual workload.

Do not assume the normal additional-dog fee alone makes the booking workable.

A larger dog household may require:

- longer Service;
- multiple Services;
- modified scope;
- custom pricing;
- or decline if safe care cannot reasonably be provided.

## 4. Large Total Pet Household

If the household includes 5 or more total pets:

- [ ] All pets are disclosed and profiled.
- [ ] All species are identified.
- [ ] Actual care workload is understood.
- [ ] Feeding and water routines are understood.
- [ ] Litter/enclosure/husbandry needs are understood.
- [ ] Medication needs are understood.
- [ ] Separation needs are understood.
- [ ] Service duration realistically fits.

Pet count is a review trigger because workload does not scale perfectly through simple per-pet fees.

Do not mechanically multiply additional-pet fees without reviewing whether the actual care plan fits.

## 5. Materially Distinct Care Types / Routines

A care type is a materially different routine requiring distinct:

- handling;
- enclosure care;
- medical planning;
- husbandry;
- or safety planning.

Species count and care-type count are not always the same.

Two species may share one simple care pattern, while two pets of the same species may create different care types where their routines or risks differ materially.

If the booking includes 3 or more materially distinct care types/routines:

- [ ] Each routine is understood.
- [ ] Separate handling requirements are understood.
- [ ] Separate enclosure/husbandry requirements are understood.
- [ ] Medication differences are understood.
- [ ] Safety differences are understood.
- [ ] Combined workload is realistic.

Use personalized review rather than automatic pricing.

## 6. Advanced / High-Risk Medication

If advanced or high-risk medication is involved:

- [ ] Medication Scope Review completed.
- [ ] Training/competence confirmed.
- [ ] Insurance/service scope confirmed.
- [ ] Timing requirements confirmed.
- [ ] Restraint requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Consequences of delay or failed administration understood.
- [ ] Required Service duration confirmed.

Do not solve advanced medical complexity by adding an arbitrary surcharge.

Possible outcomes include:

- standard Service;
- longer Service;
- modified plan;
- referral;
- or decline.

## 7. Exact-Time Care

If an exact-time requirement exists:

- [ ] Exact time is genuinely required.
- [ ] Acceptable timing window is documented.
- [ ] Travel reliability is workable.
- [ ] Route/time-block capacity is workable.
- [ ] Other confirmed Client obligations remain workable.
- [ ] Consequences of delay are understood where relevant.

Routine daytime arrival windows are not exact-minute guarantees.

Exact-time care requires review before confirmation.

Do not allow a Client to create an exact-time guarantee merely by selecting a precise time in a website form.

## 8. Significant Behavior / Safety Concern

If behavior or safety concerns are material:

- [ ] Behavior Risk Review completed.
- [ ] Required handling is workable.
- [ ] Equipment is appropriate.
- [ ] Escape risk is manageable.
- [ ] Separation is manageable.
- [ ] Sitter safety is acceptable.
- [ ] Requested Service type remains appropriate.

Do not use higher pricing to make unsafe handling acceptable.

Where safe handling cannot reasonably be assured:

- modify;
- refer;
- or decline.

## 9. Complex Separation / Predator-Prey Management

If complex separation is required:

- [ ] Rotation sequence is clearly understood.
- [ ] Doors/gates/crates/enclosures are reliable.
- [ ] Predator/prey separation is workable.
- [ ] Required handling is safe.
- [ ] Separation time fits the Service duration.
- [ ] No unsafe improvisation is required.

Complex rotation may require:

- longer Service;
- additional Service;
- revised care plan;
- custom quote;
- or decline.

Do not assume a standard additional-pet fee covers substantial separation labor.

## 10. Unusual Species / Husbandry

If unusual species or specialized husbandry is involved:

- [ ] Species is identified.
- [ ] Care requirements are understood.
- [ ] Current competence is sufficient.
- [ ] Insurance/service scope is appropriate.
- [ ] Feeding/habitat needs are understood.
- [ ] Temperature/environment needs are understood.
- [ ] Enclosure care is understood.
- [ ] Emergency pathway is workable.
- [ ] Service duration is realistic.

Unfamiliar species should trigger competence/scope review rather than automatic acceptance.

Possible outcomes include:

- accept;
- accept with modified scope;
- longer Service;
- referral to a species-specific provider;
- or decline.

## 11. Continuous / 24-Hour Care Request

If the Client requests:

- continuous presence;
- 24-hour supervision;
- a sitter who cannot leave;
- or another materially similar arrangement,

the request does not fit standard Overnight Care.

Standard Overnight Care generally covers approximately 6:00 PM–8:00 AM and is not 24-hour continuous care.

- [ ] Client expectation clarified.
- [ ] Actual required presence understood.
- [ ] Operational feasibility reviewed.
- [ ] Personal schedule impact reviewed.
- [ ] Other Client obligations reviewed.

Continuous care is not a standard launch Service.

Use personalized review and expect that the request may be unavailable or require referral.

## 12. Pet Transportation Request

Pet transportation requires personalized review.

- [ ] Transportation request identified.
- [ ] Purpose of transportation understood.
- [ ] Current insurance coverage verified.
- [ ] Current business/service scope verified.
- [ ] Authorization requirements verified.
- [ ] Appropriate alternative transportation option identified where necessary.

Do not quote or offer transportation unless current insurance and business policy explicitly permit it.

If transportation is outside coverage:

- decline that portion;
- use an authorized alternative where appropriate;
- or refer to an insured pet-transport provider.

## 13. More Than 45 Minutes Typical One-Way Travel

Travel beyond 45 minutes typical one-way requires personalized review and is generally unavailable unless specifically approved.

- [ ] Typical one-way travel time reviewed under normal conditions.
- [ ] Round-trip burden reviewed.
- [ ] Route impact reviewed.
- [ ] Other Client obligations reviewed.
- [ ] Schedule sustainability reviewed.
- [ ] Economic feasibility reviewed.

Do not accept a distant booking solely because a mathematical travel fee could be invented.

Do not extend the existing daytime travel-fee ladder beyond its approved limits without explicit authority.

## 14. Complex / Extended-Area Overnight

Overnight Care outside the Standard Zone or otherwise operationally complex requires personalized review.

Review:

- [ ] travel burden;
- [ ] evening arrival;
- [ ] morning departure;
- [ ] midday/daytime obligations;
- [ ] route compatibility;
- [ ] home suitability;
- [ ] privacy;
- [ ] access;
- [ ] behavior;
- [ ] medication;
- [ ] length of stay;
- [ ] other Client commitments.

Do not automatically apply daytime Extended or Far Extended travel fees to an Overnight booking.

The outcome may be:

- standard Overnight pricing;
- custom quote;
- modified care plan;
- referral;
- or decline.

## 15. Routine May Not Fit Within 90 Minutes

Ninety (90) minutes is the maximum standard public daytime Service duration at launch.

If the complete routine may not fit:

- [ ] Actual task list reviewed.
- [ ] Pet count reviewed.
- [ ] Care-type count reviewed.
- [ ] Medication time reviewed.
- [ ] Separation/rotation time reviewed.
- [ ] Walking/enrichment time reviewed.
- [ ] Feeding/preparation time reviewed.
- [ ] Litter/enclosure time reviewed.
- [ ] Home-task time reviewed.
- [ ] Documentation time reviewed.

Possible outcomes include:

- multiple separate Services;
- different care frequency;
- Overnight Care where appropriate;
- personalized/custom care;
- modified scope;
- referral;
- or decline.

Do not create a standard public 2-hour daytime Service merely to make the estimator work.

## 16. Actual Time / Workload Estimate

Estimate the realistic hands-on workload.

Consider:

- feeding;
- water;
- potty;
- walking;
- litter/enclosure care;
- medication;
- separation;
- behavior management;
- enrichment;
- cleanup;
- home/security tasks;
- and documentation.

Record internally:

**Actual time / workload estimate:** ______________________________

Do not artificially compress the routine to fit the Client’s requested duration.

## 17. Travel / Route Impact

Review:

- one-way typical travel;
- round-trip burden;
- location relative to other confirmed bookings;
- arrival-window feasibility;
- repeated travel across a multi-day booking;
- route density;
- and required buffer.

Record internally:

**Travel / route impact:** ______________________________

A mathematically possible quote is not acceptable if the booking makes the route operationally unrealistic.

## 18. Capacity Impact

Review capacity using:

- Service time;
- drive time;
- journal/admin time;
- realistic buffer;
- pet-specific needs;
- and other confirmed Client obligations.

- [ ] Capacity available.
- [ ] Capacity available with conditions.
- [ ] Schedule modification required.
- [ ] Capacity not realistically available.

Do not treat a visually open calendar slot as proof that capacity exists.

## 19. Welfare Review

Confirm:

- [ ] care frequency is adequate;
- [ ] requested Service duration is adequate;
- [ ] water/elimination needs are covered;
- [ ] medication needs are covered;
- [ ] companionship/monitoring needs are reasonable;
- [ ] the complete absence period is considered.

Do not reduce a care plan below what Cuddle Crew considers adequate merely to lower the quote.

Automatic feeders, cameras, fountains, and similar devices may support care but do not automatically replace appropriate in-person welfare checks.

## 20. Insurance / Service-Scope Issue

Record any relevant:

- insurance limitation;
- training limitation;
- service-scope limitation;
- legal limitation;
- transportation limitation;
- medical limitation;
- species limitation;
- or other material constraint.

**Insurance / scope issue:** ______________________________

A custom quote cannot override an insurance, legal, safety, or scope limitation.

If the requested care remains outside scope:

- modify;
- refer;
- or decline.

## 21. Determine the Correct Service Structure

Before determining a price, decide what Service structure actually fits.

Possible structures include:

- 30-Minute Drop-In;
- 60-Minute Drop-In;
- 90-Minute Extended Care;
- 30-Minute Dog Walk;
- 60-Minute Dog Walk;
- 90-Minute Adventure Walk;
- Overnight Care;
- Overnight + separate 30-minute midday care;
- Overnight + longer daytime Service;
- Overnight + Dog Walk;
- multiple daytime Services;
- or personalized/custom care.

Do not begin with a desired dollar amount and work backward into an inadequate Service plan.

## 22. Existing Standard Pricing

Apply CURRENT / APPROVED standard rates where they genuinely fit.

Custom review does not mean that every part of a booking must receive custom pricing.

For example:

- a complex household may still use normal 60-minute rates once the correct Service structure is determined;
- an Overnight may still use standard base pricing if the review confirms standard scope;
- applicable additional-pet, holiday, short-notice, or daytime travel pricing may still apply normally.

Use custom pricing only where the actual Service structure materially differs from standard pricing.

## 23. Custom Pricing Principle

If custom pricing is necessary:

- base it on actual scope;
- actual required Service time;
- travel;
- workload;
- schedule burden;
- and other legitimate operational factors.

Do not create arbitrary pricing based solely on:

- perceived risk;
- difficult personality;
- medical diagnosis;
- unusual breed;
- protected characteristics;
- or willingness to pay.

Use clean, understandable pricing.

A custom quote must never be used to make:

- unsafe;
- illegal;
- uninsurable;
- or out-of-scope care

appear acceptable.

## 24. No Risk Surcharge

Do not solve risk with a surcharge.

Use this principle:

**More time → more appropriate Service time.**

**More travel → current approved travel structure or review.**

**More visits → additional normal Services.**

**More medical complexity → Medication Scope Review.**

**More behavior risk → Behavior Risk Review.**

**More household/security complexity → access/scope review.**

**Unfamiliar species → competence/insurance/scope review.**

Risk, complexity, or uncertainty may result in:

- review;
- modification;
- referral;
- or decline.

They do not automatically justify a random extra fee.

## 25. Proposed Service / Quote

After scope review, document:

- proposed Service type(s);
- duration(s);
- frequency;
- pet pricing;
- travel treatment;
- applicable modifiers;
- custom pricing if genuinely required;
- reservation-payment requirement if applicable under current policy;
- and final total or quote structure.

**Proposed Service / Quote:** ______________________________

The quote should be understandable enough that the Client can see what Services are actually being proposed.

## 26. Quote vs. Booking Acceptance

A custom quote does not guarantee:

- calendar availability;
- booking acceptance;
- final confirmation;
- or that outstanding onboarding requirements have been completed.

The booking still requires applicable:

- Client onboarding;
- Meet & Greet;
- agreements;
- authorizations;
- access readiness;
- payment requirements;
- and final confirmation.

Pricing and acceptance are related but not identical decisions.

## 27. Website Estimate vs. Custom Quote

A website estimate is preliminary.

A custom quote produced after human review is the booking-stage pricing decision.

The website estimate must not override:

- personalized review;
- final approved scope;
- Precise Petcare quote;
- invoice;
- or confirmed booking.

Where the estimator reaches a review trigger, the correct website behavior is ordinarily:

**Personalized review required**

rather than inventing a definitive price.

## 28. Referral Review

Referral may be appropriate when the Client’s needs exceed current scope.

Potential referral categories include:

- veterinary technician / medical-needs pet sitter;
- veterinarian;
- trainer / behavior consultant;
- veterinary behavior professional;
- species-specific / exotic pet sitter;
- boarding or veterinary facility;
- groomer;
- insured pet-transport provider;
- continuous-care provider.

Referral does not imply endorsement of a specific provider unless Cuddle Crew has an established verified relationship.

## 29. Decline Review

Decline when the plan remains:

- unsafe;
- outside current Service scope;
- outside insurance coverage;
- unlawful;
- incompatible with animal welfare;
- or operationally unrealistic

after reasonable review and possible modification.

Examples may include:

- Client refuses a necessary safe care plan;
- severe handling risk cannot be mitigated;
- transportation is not covered;
- required route/capacity is impossible;
- continuous care is unavailable;
- or specialized care remains outside competence.

Do not quote a price for care Cuddle Crew should not provide.

## 30. Decision

Select one:

- [ ] **QUOTE**
- [ ] **QUOTE WITH CONDITIONS**
- [ ] **MODIFY**
- [ ] **PERSONALIZED REVIEW STILL REQUIRED**
- [ ] **REFER**
- [ ] **DECLINE**
- [ ] **PENDING INFORMATION**

### Reason

Record internally the operational reason for the decision.

Possible categories include:

- time;
- workload;
- care frequency;
- behavior;
- medication;
- separation;
- species;
- travel;
- route;
- capacity;
- Overnight scope;
- insurance;
- Service scope;
- safety;
- or another relevant factor.

Avoid unnecessary subjective commentary.

## 31. Quote Conditions

Where a quote is conditional, document applicable conditions such as:

- longer Service required;
- minimum visit frequency;
- access change;
- equipment requirement;
- medication condition;
- separation plan;
- exact-time approval;
- transportation exclusion;
- travel limitation;
- Meet & Greet completion;
- updated forms;
- or another prerequisite.

A condition should address an actual operational requirement rather than function as a disguised arbitrary surcharge.

## 32. Custom Quote Principle

The purpose of Custom Quote / Scope Review is to determine the **correct care plan first and the correct price second**.

Do not auto-quote when material uncertainty exists about:

- time;
- risk;
- scope;
- welfare;
- travel;
- complexity;
- or operational feasibility.

A custom quote is appropriate only after the requested care has been defined well enough to determine that Cuddle Crew can responsibly provide it.

## Website Implementation Notes

This document should control when automated pricing logic stops.

The full internal review rubric must not be exposed publicly.

### Primary Website Rule

If a request clearly fits standard Service rules, the website may calculate an estimate.

If a request hits a CURRENT / APPROVED custom-review trigger, the website should stop short of presenting a definitive final price and use:

**Personalized review required**

### Review Trigger Logic

Internal website logic may detect:

- 4+ dogs;
- 5+ total pets;
- 3+ materially distinct care types/routines;
- advanced/high-risk medication;
- exact-time care;
- significant behavior/safety concern;
- complex separation/predator-prey;
- unusual species/husbandry;
- continuous/24-hour care;
- pet transportation;
- >45-minute typical one-way travel;
- complex/extended-area Overnight;
- routine that may not fit within 90 minutes.

These triggers may exist in backend decision logic.

Do not publish the complete threshold table unless explicitly approved.

### Public Result

Preferred client-facing wording:

**Personalized review required**

Optional explanatory text:

**This care plan needs a quick individual review before we can confirm the right Service and pricing.**

Do not expose internal labels such as:

- high risk;
- too many pets;
- route burden;
- medical risk;
- unsafe household;
- or capacity failure.

### Preliminary Estimate Where Appropriate

A review trigger does not necessarily mean the website must hide all price information.

Where standard portions of the booking can still be estimated accurately, the website may show:

**Estimated starting price**

or:

**Preliminary estimate**

alongside:

**Final Service plan and pricing require personalized review.**

Do not label that amount as final.

### Definitive Price

The website should only present a definitive final quote where:

- scope is sufficiently clear;
- standard or approved custom pricing has been determined;
- applicable modifiers are known;
- and human review has resolved the trigger.

### Duration Logic

Do not create a standard 120-minute daytime Service to avoid review.

Current public daytime ladder is:

- 30 minutes;
- 60 minutes;
- 90 minutes;
- personalized/custom review.

### Additional-Pet Logic

Do not assume:

`base rate + additional-pet fees = acceptable booking`

when pet count or workload triggers review.

A calculated amount can still require personalized review.

### Care-Type Logic

Website implementation may count materially distinct care routines internally where sufficient structured data exists.

Do not infer care-type count solely from species count.

Where care-type classification is uncertain, route to human review.

### Medical Logic

Advanced/high-risk or exact-time medication should route through Medication Scope Review.

Do not create an automatic medication-risk surcharge.

### Behavior Logic

Significant behavior/safety concerns should route through Behavior Risk Review.

Do not auto-price behavior risk.

### Travel Logic

For normal daytime travel, use the CURRENT / APPROVED Pricing, Fees & Surcharge Policy.

More than 45 minutes typical one-way travel must route to personalized review.

Do not invent a >45-minute automatic surcharge.

### Overnight Logic

Complex or extended-area Overnight requests require personalized review.

Do not automatically apply daytime travel surcharges to Overnight Care.

### Continuous Care

Continuous or 24-hour care is not a standard launch Service.

Do not let the website create a standard continuous-care price.

Route to personalized review or indicate that the Service may be unavailable.

### Transportation

Do not quote transportation unless CURRENT / APPROVED insurance and Service-scope references confirm that it is available.

A transportation request should stop automatic finalization.

### Capacity

The estimator must not treat a technically open calendar slot as guaranteed capacity.

Internal capacity may include:

- Service time;
- drive time;
- journal/admin;
- buffer;
- and existing confirmed Clients.

Do not expose these calculations publicly.

### Welfare

Do not let the estimator reduce:

- visit frequency;
- Service duration;
- or required care

below approved welfare standards simply to produce a cheaper result.

### Custom Quotes

If a human-approved custom quote is created, store or transfer the final Client-specific quote through the approved Precise Petcare workflow.

Do not hardcode one-off Client pricing into public frontend logic.

### No Arbitrary Fees

Do not generate:

- risk surcharge;
- medical-complexity surcharge;
- behavior surcharge;
- unusual-species surcharge;
- continuous-care surcharge;
- or >45-minute surcharge

unless a future CURRENT / APPROVED pricing policy expressly establishes one.

### Centralized Logic

Prefer a centralized authoritative configuration/data layer for:

- standard rates;
- modifiers;
- travel tiers;
- review triggers;
- and public estimate behavior.

Avoid duplicating these rules across multiple components.

### Public vs. Private Logic

Keep separate:

**Public display logic**
- what Services/rates are shown;
- estimate messaging;
- simple disclosures.

**Private review logic**
- internal thresholds;
- scope assessment;
- route/capacity reasoning;
- risk analysis;
- referral/decline reasoning.

Do not leak private review logic into frontend source, analytics, or client-visible API responses where avoidable.

### Precise Petcare Handoff

The website estimate is not the authoritative booking record.

Final Client-specific:

- Services;
- durations;
- pricing;
- modifiers;
- dates;
- scope;
- and approval conditions

should be reflected in Precise Petcare.

### Conflict Handling

If website logic conflicts with this CURRENT / APPROVED Custom Quote / Scope Review:

1. flag the conflict;
2. identify the affected estimator or booking logic;
3. preserve the internal review requirement;
4. do not weaken a review trigger merely to produce an automatic quote.

If this document conflicts with a CURRENT / APPROVED Client-facing policy, the Client-facing policy controls the contractual subject and the conflict should be flagged for human review.
