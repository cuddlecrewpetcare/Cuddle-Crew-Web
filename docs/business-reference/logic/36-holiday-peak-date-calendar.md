## Purpose

This document is the annual source of truth for the exact holiday and designated peak-date periods used by Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC.

Once populated and marked `CURRENT / APPROVED`, this document will determine exactly which Service dates or periods qualify for applicable:

- holiday daytime surcharges;
- holiday Overnight surcharges;
- holiday cancellation rules;
- holiday/peak-date booking disclosures;
- estimate-builder holiday logic;
- booking-review logic;
- and other holiday-specific website or operational behavior.

The names of possible holiday periods listed in this document do **not** by themselves establish qualifying dates.

A holiday or peak-date period is authoritative only when:

1. the exact start date/time is defined;
2. the exact end date/time is defined;
3. the applicable daytime and/or Overnight treatment is identified;
4. the period has been intentionally approved by Cuddle Crew;
5. and this document is marked `CURRENT / APPROVED`.

Until then, website logic must not infer or invent qualifying holiday dates.

## Overnight Date Rule

Unless an approved published holiday period explicitly states otherwise, use the **calendar date on which the Overnight begins** to determine whether the Overnight holiday surcharge applies.

Example structure:

- Overnight begins on a qualifying holiday date → that Overnight may receive the applicable holiday surcharge.
- Overnight begins on a non-qualifying date → no holiday Overnight surcharge solely because the Overnight extends into the following calendar date.

If Cuddle Crew intends a multi-day holiday period to work differently, the start and end of that period must be explicitly defined in this calendar before publication.

Do not infer a multi-day period from the holiday name alone.

## Multi-Day Period Rule

Where Cuddle Crew uses a multi-day holiday or peak period:

- define the exact first qualifying date/time;
- define the exact final qualifying date/time;
- identify whether daytime Services qualify;
- identify whether Overnight Services qualify;
- and publish the period before applying it to an affected booking.

Do not use vague production rules such as:

- “Thanksgiving week”;
- “Christmas weekend”;
- “holiday season”;
- “New Year’s weekend”;
- or “major holidays”

without exact qualifying dates being defined.

## Date-Boundary Rule

Holiday logic must use the expressly approved calendar boundaries.

Do not automatically extend a period because:

- the holiday falls on a weekend;
- a federal observance occurs on another weekday;
- schools are closed;
- businesses are closed;
- travel demand is high;
- or a neighboring pet-care business uses a longer holiday period.

Only the dates approved in this document qualify.

### Estimator Logic

Once approved, the estimate builder may use this calendar to determine whether an estimated Service falls within a qualifying holiday period.

The estimator must:

1. determine the Service date;
2. compare it to CURRENT / APPROVED holiday-period data;
3. determine whether the Service type qualifies;
4. apply the surcharge amount from the CURRENT / APPROVED Pricing Policy;
5. clearly disclose the holiday modifier in the estimate.

The calendar provides the **dates**.

The Pricing Policy provides the **amounts**.

Do not duplicate the dollar amounts into a second independent pricing authority if centralized pricing data can be used.

### Daytime Services

For a daytime Service:

- evaluate the actual calendar date/time of the Service against the approved holiday period;
- apply the holiday daytime modifier only where the approved period says daytime pricing applies.

Do not assume every holiday period applies identically to daytime and Overnight Services.

### Overnight Services

Unless an approved period states otherwise:

- use the calendar date on which the Overnight begins.

Do not determine the holiday surcharge based solely on:

- the date the Overnight ends;
- whether midnight occurs during the stay;
- or whether part of the Overnight overlaps a holiday date.

If a multi-day holiday period is approved, use its explicit defined boundaries.

### Cancellation Logic

Website cancellation tools must use:

1. this calendar to determine whether the affected booking falls within an approved holiday/peak period;

This calendar does not independently determine cancellation charges.

### No Dynamic Holiday Expansion

Do not dynamically expand holiday periods because of:

- demand;
- availability;
- weekend proximity;
- observed federal holidays;
- school schedules;
- traffic;
- travel trends;
- or third-party holiday calendars.

Changing the qualifying period is a business-policy change and requires updating this authoritative file.

### Time Zone

Holiday-date calculations should use the business’s applicable local time zone consistently.

Do not allow browser, server, or Client travel time zones to silently shift whether a local Service date qualifies.

### Historical Bookings

Future changes to the holiday calendar should not silently recalculate already confirmed booking pricing contrary to the CURRENT / APPROVED Pricing Policy.

Confirmed pricing remains subject to the rules governing confirmed bookings.

### Precise Petcare Alignment

- configure them to match the CURRENT / APPROVED calendar;
- do not maintain a contradictory independent holiday schedule;
- flag any PPC limitation that prevents exact alignment.

This document remains the business-rule source for which holiday periods are intended to qualify.
