## Purpose

This document defines the annual business and policy audit process for Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC.

The purpose of the audit is to verify that the business’s actual operations remain aligned across:

- internal policies;
- Client-facing agreements;
- pricing;
- website content;
- public claims;
- service descriptions;
- insurance;
- licensing;
- credentials;
- service area;
- scheduling capacity;
- software;
- privacy/security;
- emergency procedures;
- and operational systems.

This audit is intended to catch drift before outdated or inaccurate information remains in production systems or Client-facing materials.

This document is an internal operational reference.

It may inform internal administrative tools, website-maintenance workflows, documentation processes, reminders, and compliance checks, but the full audit record and internal corrective-action notes should not be publicly exposed.

## Authoritative Content

# ANNUAL BUSINESS & POLICY AUDIT

Complete this audit at least annually and additionally whenever a material business change makes an earlier review appropriate.

Examples of material changes may include:

- pricing changes;
- new Services;
- removed Services;
- insurance changes;
- business-license changes;
- new certifications;
- expired credentials;
- service-area changes;
- major website changes;
- payment-policy changes;
- new software;
- privacy/security changes;
