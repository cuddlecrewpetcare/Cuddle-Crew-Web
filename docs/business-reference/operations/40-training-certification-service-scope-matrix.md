## Purpose

This document is the internal source of truth for Cuddle Crew Pet Care LLC’s current training, certifications, credentials, insurance-confirmed capabilities, and Service-scope limitations.

Its purpose is to prevent the website, marketing materials, booking logic, estimate builder, Client-facing forms, or other systems from representing a capability that has not actually been verified.

For each relevant Service or care area, this matrix should record:

- applicable training, certification, or credential;
- completion date;
- expiration or renewal date where applicable;
- whether applicable insurance coverage has been confirmed;
- current approved Service scope;
- limitations;
- and whether the capability may be represented publicly.

This document must remain `PLACEHOLDER` until the applicable rows have been deliberately populated and verified.

A blank row does not mean that Cuddle Crew is qualified, unqualified, insured, or uninsured for that activity.

A blank row means:

**Current status has not yet been established in this authoritative matrix.**

The public website must not infer capabilities from blank fields.

## 13. Public Credential Claim Rule

A credential or training claim may be represented publicly only when:

- the credential/course has actually been completed;
- the exact credential name is known;
- the issuing organization is known;
- the credential remains current where expiration applies;
- the wording does not exaggerate the credential;
- and the claim has been approved for public use.

Do not describe:

- membership as certification;
- course attendance as licensure;
- planned training as completed;
- unrelated human medical training as veterinary qualification;
- or informal experience as a formal credential.

## 14. Service Capability Rule

A Service capability may be represented publicly only when:

- current competence is sufficient;
- training is appropriate where needed;
- applicable insurance has been confirmed;
- the Service fits the business’s approved scope;
- relevant safety procedures exist;
- and any required Client-facing policy or authorization is in place.

All of those questions are separate.

For example:

**Training exists** does not automatically mean **insurance covers it**.

**Insurance covers it** does not automatically mean **Cuddle Crew is trained to perform it**.

**Cuddle Crew is capable of performing it** does not automatically mean **it is currently offered as a business Service**.

## Website Implementation Notes

This matrix should act as a gatekeeper for public capability and credential claims.

While the document remains `PLACEHOLDER`, Codex must not treat blank rows as authorization to add or expand Services.

### Public Credential Display

Website sections displaying:

- certifications;
- training;
- memberships;
- professional development;
- or specialty qualifications

should use only verified, approved information.

Do not automatically scrape or expose every internal training entry.

### Credential Status

Where a public credential has an expiration or renewal requirement, website/admin logic may track whether it is:

- Current
- Expiring
- Expired

Do not publicly display `Current` unless verified.

### Service Availability

Where practical, internal Service availability may be tied to an approved scope status.

For example:

- `APPROVED — STANDARD` → may appear in normal Service selection.
- `APPROVED — CONDITIONAL` → may require qualifying questions.
- `PERSONALIZED REVIEW REQUIRED` → should not auto-confirm.
- `NOT CURRENTLY OFFERED` → should not appear as bookable.
- `OUT OF SCOPE` → must not be offered.
- `PENDING INSURANCE VERIFICATION` → must not be offered.

The exact technical enum names may differ.

The business meaning must remain the same.

### Medication

Do not let the website infer that all medication care is available from a broad statement such as:

**Medication administration available.**

If medication capability is displayed publicly, wording should reflect the actual approved scope.

Complex cases still route through Medication Scope Review.

### Pet Transportation

Until the transportation row is fully verified and approved:

- do not display pet transportation as a Service;
- do not include transportation in automatic quotes;
- do not let a Client select transportation as an available add-on;
- do not advertise transportation capability.

A transportation inquiry should route to personalized review.

### Species

Do not publish support for an entire species category merely because one related species or husbandry routine has been approved.

Where exact scope is narrow, public wording should remain accurate.

### Planned Training

The website may not turn an internal `PLANNED` or `IN PROGRESS` credential into a completed public claim.

Do not display:

- “Certified in...”
- “Trained in...”
- “Specialized in...”

unless the matrix supports that exact claim.

### Membership vs. Certification

Professional membership data should come from the appropriate authoritative source.

Do not convert PSI membership or another membership into a certification claim.

### Public Wording Field

Where a capability or credential has nuanced limitations, prefer storing approved public wording rather than letting individual components invent their own descriptions.

This can prevent inconsistent claims across:

- homepage;
- about page;
- Service pages;
- FAQ;
- SEO metadata;
- structured data;
- social sharing metadata;
- and booking UX.

### Structured Data / SEO

Do not add qualifications, credentials, awards, or Service capabilities to structured data unless they are supported by CURRENT / APPROVED references.

Search-engine metadata is still a public business claim.

### Precise Petcare Alignment

Services enabled in Precise Petcare should remain compatible with the approved scope in this matrix.

If Precise Petcare contains a Service that this matrix marks:

- `NOT CURRENTLY OFFERED`;
- `OUT OF SCOPE`;
- or `PENDING INSURANCE VERIFICATION`;

flag the conflict.

Do not silently treat software configuration as authority.

### Annual Audit Integration

The Annual Business & Policy Audit should verify that:

- matrix status is current;
- credentials have not expired;
- insurance scope has not changed;
- website claims still match;
- Precise Petcare Service options still match;
- and no unsupported Service has been added.

### Conflict Handling

If website behavior or public claims conflict with this matrix after it becomes `CURRENT / APPROVED`:

1. flag the conflict;

3. preserve the verified Service limitation;
4. do not expand capability merely to match existing website code.

If another CURRENT / APPROVED source says a Service is offered but this matrix says the necessary training or insurance is not verified, treat that as a material conflict requiring human review before offering the Service.
