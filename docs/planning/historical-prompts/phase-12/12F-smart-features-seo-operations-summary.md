### Client preparation

Potential resources covered preparing for a pet sitter, home access, emergency contacts, food/supply locations, medication-instruction preparation, smart feeders, pet cameras, GPS collars, automatic litter boxes, smart doors, and technology backup instructions. This did not authorize surveillance integrations.

### Safety education and continuity

Potential educational resources covered Sacramento heat, wildfire smoke/air quality, weather safety, visit duration/frequency, and choosing a professional sitter, without veterinary diagnosis or advice.

Public continuity explanations were intended to remain truthful and bounded around emergency procedures, veterinary authorization, home-access failure, sitter illness/emergency, and continuity planning. They must never invent backup personnel.

### Accessibility statement and contact reliability

Evaluate only a truthful accessibility statement/contact method and do not overclaim compliance. Audit form success/failure, email and portal fallback, phone and SMS wording, and texting-availability claims.

### SEO and local discoverability

Historical review areas included titles, meta descriptions, H1s, canonical URLs, internal links, alt text, LocalBusiness and WebSite structured data, Sacramento/Carmichael relevance, service-area wording, Services and Service Area hubs, crawlability, OpenGraph, 404 behavior, sitemap, robots, and `sameAs`. Avoid thin city-page spam.

### Trust and claims

Audit support for reviews, ratings, clients, years in business, certifications, training, background checks, insurance, bonding, employees, backup sitters, availability, transportation, medical expertise, 24/7 service, and PSI.

Historical 12F also included a broad privacy/security audit.

- auto-booking;
- duplicate CRM, Client database, or login/account system;
- exact private Client-location storage or a public private calendar;
- unsupported live availability;
- pet-camera integrations or GPS tracking;
- transportation workflow unless approved;
- fake reviews, certifications, or personnel;
- pointless gamification.
